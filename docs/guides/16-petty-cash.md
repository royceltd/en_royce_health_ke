# Petty Cash — Step-by-Step Guide

How staff ask for petty cash, how the accounts office approves and pays it, and how the float is topped up. Shown on the demo hospital at hospital.royceltd.com.

## At a glance

| Step | Who | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Request | Any staff member | Lists what is needed (item, quantity, price), for themselves or on someone's behalf, and submits it |
| 2. Approve or reject | Accounts office | Approves (no amount limit), or rejects with a reason. Nobody approves their own request |
| 3. Pay | Accounts office | Hands over the cash: the payment is booked from the petty cash float to the expense accounts, and the voucher is printed and signed |
| Top up | Accounts office | Refills the float from the bank or M-Pesa |

The float is the **Petty Cash** account. It is made the first time it is needed, next to the company's cash account, and can be changed in Kenya Accounting Settings › *Petty Cash Account*.

## Step 1 — Request petty cash

**Who:** any staff member. **Where:** Kenya Accounting › Petty Cash › **Petty Cash Request** › *+ Add*.

1. Under **What is needed**, add a line for each thing: what it is for, the quantity and the price per unit. The amount and the total are worked out. Choose an **Item** if there is one for it (for example a vehicle expense); it fills in the account.
2. Write the **Purpose**.
3. Asking for someone else (a ward, a colleague)? Tick **Request on behalf of someone** and say who.
4. Save, then **Submit**. The request is *Pending Approval*.

Staff see only their own requests. The accounts office sees all of them.

Demo record: the receptionist's request for printer toner, waiting for approval.

## Step 2 — Approve or reject

**Who:** accounts office (roles *Accounts User* or *Accounts Manager*). **Where:** the Petty Cash Request list, filtered to *Pending Approval*.

1. Open the request. The bar at the top shows how much the petty cash float holds.
2. Check each line's **Expense Account**, and fill in any the requester left blank (Stationery, Fuel, Repairs…). The accounts can be corrected until the request is paid.
3. **Approve** (with an optional remark), or **Reject** and say why.

There is no amount limit. You can't approve a request you made yourself: another person in the accounts office does.

Demo record: the night nurses' tea and sugar, approved and waiting to be paid.

## Step 3 — Pay

**Who:** accounts office. **Where:** the approved request › **Pay**.

1. Enter who is receiving the cash, and the date.
2. **Pay**. The payment is booked: each line's expense account is debited and the petty cash account credited (a Journal Entry, linked on the request). The request is *Paid*.
3. Print the **Petty Cash Voucher** (the print icon). The person signs for the cash under *Received by*.

A payment larger than the float holds is refused: top up first. If a payment was made by mistake, cancel its Journal Entry: the request goes back to *Approved*.

Demo record: the pharmacy's courier request, paid.

## Top up the float

**Who:** accounts office. **Where:** the Petty Cash Request list › **Top Up Petty Cash**.

Choose the bank or M-Pesa account the money comes from, the amount, and the cheque number or M-Pesa code. The list shows the float's new balance.

## Reports

- **General Ledger** for the Petty Cash account: the petty cash book, every top-up and payment.
- **General Ledger** or **Profit and Loss** by expense account: what petty cash was spent on.
