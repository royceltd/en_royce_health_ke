# No Consultation or Triage Before Payment — Testing Guide

A short script for version 0.41, your IT team's finding of 17 November: a doctor could open a consultation, and triage could take vital signs, for a cash patient still waiting at the Cashier. Now the rule holds everywhere, not only for **Send To**.

**Test with the role logins** (Reception, Cashier, Nurse, Doctor), not Administrator. Administrator skips some checks by design (for example "only cashiers take payment"), so it doesn't show what each department sees.

Test on the demo (**hospital.royceltd.com**) or your own system. Use new test patients with "Test" in the name. Each test says what to do and what the system should do; tick it off, and send a screenshot if anything differs.

## Before you start (manager, once)

Kenya Health Settings › Payment Before Service:
- [ ] **Consultation fee paid first** is ticked, and **Consultation fee is paid** = *Before triage*.
- [ ] **Consultations only for checked-in patients** is ticked.

## A. Before paying, nobody sees the patient

1. **Reception:** check in a cash test patient (*Outpatient*). The visit waits at the **Cashier**.
2. **Doctor:** Consultation › **+ Add**, and choose that patient.
   - [ ] A red notice at the top at once: the patient has not paid the consultation fee.
   - [ ] **Save** is refused with the same message.
3. **Nurse:** Vital Signs › **+ Add** for that patient, and Save.
   - [ ] Refused: the consultation fee is not paid.
4. **Cashier:** take the fee (visit › Bill › Create Bill › Create Invoice, save, **Take Payment**).
   - [ ] The patient moves to **Triage** by themselves.
5. **Nurse:** record the vital signs again.
   - [ ] Saved.
6. **Doctor:** open the consultation again.
   - [ ] No red notice; it saves.

## B. Not checked in

1. **Doctor:** Consultation › **+ Add** for a patient **not checked in today**.
   - [ ] Red notice: not checked in, check the patient in at reception first. **Save** is refused.

## C. Who is still seen before paying

- [ ] An **insured** patient: no notice; triage and the doctor see them at once.
- [ ] A **Casualty** visit: the same.
- [ ] A patient a **manager** let through (visit › Bill › **Allow Services Before Payment**): the doctor can open the consultation.

## D. Fee before the doctor (optional)

For hospitals that triage first and take the fee before the doctor.

1. **Manager:** set **Consultation fee is paid** = *Before the doctor*.
2. **Reception:** check in a cash test patient.
   - [ ] The patient goes straight to **Triage**; the nurse can record vital signs.
3. **Nurse:** **Send To** › *Consultation*.
   - [ ] The patient goes to the **Cashier** first ("the consultation fee is not paid").
4. **Cashier:** take the fee.
   - [ ] The patient moves to **Consultation**, to the doctor chosen at check-in.
5. **Manager:** set it back to *Before triage*.
