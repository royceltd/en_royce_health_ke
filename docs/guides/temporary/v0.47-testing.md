# Inpatient: Admission, the Ward, the Patient Screen, Ward Drugs, Nursing Charts and Discharge — Testing Guide

This is a testing script for version 0.47.0, the inpatient module in the Bethesda proposals: a patient admitted from the consultation, received on the ward, cared for (vitals, notes, charts, ward rounds, medicines given) and discharged with a full summary.

Test on the demo, **hospital.royceltd.com**. Use the role logins: Doctor, Nurse, Pharmacist, Cashier, and Administrator for part A. Tick each item as you go, and send a screenshot if anything is different.

## A. Setup (Administrator)

1. **Wards and beds.** The demo has *General Ward* with beds *GW Bed 01* to *04* (Healthcare Service Units: the ward is a group, each bed a unit with **Inpatient Occupancy** and a bed type that carries the daily charge).
   - [ ] Kenya Health › Inpatient › **Ward** opens, and *General Ward* is in the Ward filter.
2. Kenya Health Settings › **Inpatient (Wards)**:
   - **Charge ward drugs**: leave *On issue* for now (part F tries both);
   - **Deposit Asked at Admission (cash)**: *5,000*;
   - **When the bill passes what is paid**: *Warn*;
   - **Nursing Shifts**: *Day* 07:00–19:00 and *Night* 19:00–07:00 are there.
3. Kenya Health › Inpatient › **Nursing Chart Template**.
   - [ ] *Feeding Chart*, *Blood Transfusion* and *Hygiene and Turning* are there. Open *Blood Transfusion*: each column with its unit and alert range, and **Second Nurse Checks Each Entry** ticked.
4. Type *Patient Workspace Profile* in the search bar and open *Inpatient*.
   - [ ] Ten tabs in this order: Patient File, Vitals, Requests, Results, Diagnosis, Nursing Notes, Nursing Care, Drug Administration, Billing, Discharge. Renaming or hiding a tab here changes the patient screen, no release needed.
5. One insurer: on an **Insurance Payor** (Insurance › Insurance Payor), tick **Admissions Need Pre-authorisation** with *12* hours (part I).

## B. Admit to Ward (Doctor)

Use a cash patient who is with the doctor (checked in, triaged, consultation open).

1. On the consultation, press **Admit to Ward**.
   - [ ] A box asks for **Ward**, **Urgency** (Routine, Urgent, Emergency), **Admission Date**, **Expected Stay**, **Admission Reason** and **Admission Notes**.
2. Press **Admit** without a reason.
   - [ ] Refused: give the reason.
3. Fill everything (*General Ward*, *Urgent*, today, reason *Severe malaria, unable to keep oral drugs*, notes *Start IV fluids*), **Admit**.
   - [ ] "Admission requested". The patient's visit is now at **Admission**.
4. Press **Admit to Ward** again for the same patient.
   - [ ] Refused: the patient already has an admission.
5. With a lab test still waiting for its result, press **Complete Consultation**.
   - [ ] It completes: the result follows the patient to the ward.

## C. The ward receives the patient (Nurse)

1. Kenya Health › Inpatient › **Ward**.
   - [ ] Under **Waiting for a bed**: the patient, *Urgent* in orange, the ward, the reason and the date. Emergencies are listed first.
2. Press **Give Bed** and choose *GW Bed 01*.
   - [ ] The patient's screen opens. Back on the Ward page, they are under **On the ward** with their bed, days and doctor; the bed is no longer offered to the next patient.

## D. The patient screen (Nurse, Doctor)

1. Open the patient from the Ward page.
   - [ ] The banner shows the patient, age and sex, ward and bed, admission date and days, doctor, payer, allergies (in red) and the admission reason.
   - [ ] A cash patient whose bill is above what is paid shows an orange note with the running bill, what is paid and the deposit asked.
   - [ ] The ten tabs run across the top.
2. **Patient File**: **Open Patient File** opens the whole record; **Admitting Consultation** opens the doctor's consultation.
3. **Vitals**: **Record Vital Signs** opens Marley's Nursing Dialog on *Vitals & Pain*. Record a set, close the dialog.
   - [ ] The reading is listed, newest first, abnormal ones flagged in red.
4. **Nursing Notes**: **Add Nursing Note** (the admission assessment). Close.
   - [ ] The note shows with the time, the **shift** (Day or Night), who wrote it and its type. **Shift Handover** and **Nursing Tasks** open their panes.
5. **Results** and **Diagnosis** show this admission's lab results, imaging and diagnoses with ICD-11 codes.

## E. Ward round: prescribing and requests (Doctor)

1. **Requests** tab › **Ward Round: new requests or medicines**.
   - [ ] A new consultation opens, already linked to the admission.
2. Prescribe *Tablet Paracetamol 500mg*, *TID*, *3 Day*; add a lab test; enter a diagnosis; **Complete Consultation**.
   - [ ] No Cashier step: inpatients are served first and settle at discharge.
3. Back on the patient screen › **Drug Administration**.
   - [ ] Under **Active prescriptions**: paracetamol with *9 waiting for pharmacy* (three a day for three days, at 06:00, 14:00 and 22:00) and the next dose time.
4. **Requests** tab.
   - [ ] The lab test is listed with where it goes (*Laboratory*) and its status.

## F. Pharmacy sends the drugs; the nurse gives them (Pharmacist, Nurse)

1. **Pharmacist:** Pharmacy Worklist › choose the **Pharmacy store** › **Ward Requests**.
   - [ ] The patient is listed with their ward and bed: paracetamol, the doses due in the next 24 hours and the quantity.
2. Press **Issue to Ward**.
   - [ ] "Sent to the ward". The patient leaves the list.
3. **Nurse:** patient screen › **Drug Administration**.
   - [ ] The doses due today now count as sent (charged on issue: *given* once issued).
4. **Medication Round** opens Marley's medication pane: the doses due, with **Given**, **Held**, **Refused**. Mark one **Given** and one **Held** with a reason.
   - [ ] Under **Treatment sheet** both show, with the time, the nurse and the comment.
5. **Charged on administration:** Administrator sets **Charge ward drugs** to *On administration*. Each bed needs a warehouse (Healthcare Service Unit › Warehouse). Repeat steps 1 to 4 for a new prescription.
   - [ ] Now the drugs reach the bed *at the bedside*, and each dose is charged only when it is marked Given. Leftovers can be returned to the pharmacy from the admission record at discharge.
6. **Doctor:** on a drug with doses left, press **Stop** and give a reason.
   - [ ] Its remaining doses show *stopped*, and they no longer appear on the pharmacy's Ward Requests.

## G. Nursing care and charts (Nurse)

1. **Nursing Care** tab.
   - [ ] **Fluid balance, last 24 hours**: in, out and balance (from Marley's Intake / Output, recorded with the **Intake / Output** button).
   - [ ] Each chart (Feeding, Blood Transfusion, Hygiene and Turning) has **Add Entry**.
2. Feeding Chart › **Add Entry**: route *Oral*, amount *150*, tolerated *Yes*. Then another of *100*.
   - [ ] Both listed with the time, shift and nurse; **Last 24 hours: Amount 250**.
3. Feeding Chart with no route.
   - [ ] Refused: the route is required.
4. Blood Transfusion › **Add Entry** with temperature *38.6* and no second nurse.
   - [ ] Refused: a second nurse checks. Choose another nurse in **Checked by** and save: the temperature shows in red and the second nurse is shown.
5. Hygiene and Turning shows **Next due** two hours after the last entry.

## H. Billing (Cashier, Nurse)

1. Patient screen › **Billing**.
   - [ ] **Running bill**, **Billed**, **Not yet billed**, **Paid** and **Owed on bills**; under *Not yet billed*: the bed days at the bed's rate, the drugs issued, and the lab test.
2. **Cashier:** take a deposit of 5,000 against the patient (Payment Entry, received from the patient's customer).
   - [ ] **Paid** shows it; the orange note goes once the bill is below what is paid.
3. Administrator sets **When the bill passes what is paid** to *Warn and notify billing*.
   - [ ] The next morning the Cashier and the Healthcare Administrator get a notification naming the admissions above what is paid.

## I. Pre-authorisation (Doctor, Cashier)

1. Admit an **insured** patient whose insurer needs pre-authorisation (part A, step 5).
   - [ ] On the Ward page and the patient screen: *Pre-auth pending*, with when it is due. Care goes on as normal.
2. Make the admission's insurance claim and submit it.
   - [ ] Refused: the admission is waiting for the payer's pre-authorisation code.
3. On the patient screen, **Record the code** (*PA-123*).
   - [ ] The warning goes, and the claim can be submitted.

## J. Discharge (Doctor, Nurse)

1. **Discharge** tab › **Plan Discharge**: today, a follow-up date and instructions.
   - [ ] The status is *Discharge Scheduled*; the Ward page shows *Discharge planned*.
2. **Write Discharge Summary**: fill the examination, treatment and advice; save and submit.
3. **Print Discharge Summary**.
   - [ ] Besides the doctor's text, it shows: the reason for admission, the diagnoses during the stay with ICD-11 codes, the vital signs (count, abnormal, the latest), medicines given on the ward (given and held counts), investigations with results, procedures, nursing care (chart entries and the latest notes by shift) and the bill (total, paid, owed).
4. **Discharge (admission record)** › Marley's **Discharge**.
   - [ ] Marley checks nursing tasks, the bill and open requests before discharging. The bed becomes vacant again.
5. Open the patient's **Patient File**.
   - [ ] The admission is listed; its records stay in the patient's history.
