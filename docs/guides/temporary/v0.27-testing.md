# Lab Worklist and Results Inbox — Testing Guide

A short script to test version 0.27: the laboratory's worklist (one row per patient order, tests inside), collecting samples, entering and validating results, walk-in requests, and the doctors' Results Inbox. Each test says what to do and what the system should do. Tick each one off. If anything behaves differently, send a screenshot.

Test on the demo hospital (hospital.royceltd.com), not on your live site, because the tests create patients, visits, invoices and lab tests.

How it works day to day: [Outpatient guide](../02-outpatient.md), Steps 6 and 7.

## Before you start

You need four logins (Royce can create them for you on the demo):

| Login | Roles | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reception | Receptionist | Check-in, new patients |
| Doctor | Physician, linked to Dr Amina Achieng (her **User** field) | Ordering tests, Results Inbox |
| Lab | Laboratory User | The Lab Worklist |
| Cashier | Cashier | Bills and payments |

Write down the patient, visit (VIS-…) and lab test (HLC-LAB-…) numbers you create.

## A. One row per order

1. **Reception:** check in a new cash patient for an outpatient consultation with Dr Amina.
2. **Doctor:** in the consultation, order three tests (malaria slide, Hb, WBC) and submit.
3. **Lab:** open Kenya Health › Laboratory › **Lab Worklist**.
   - [ ] The *Requests* tab shows **one** row for the patient, with *3* tests named, *Cash*, and *3 not paid*.
4. Click the row.
   - [ ] The three tests are listed, each *Requested* and *Not paid*, and none can be ticked.
   - [ ] **Collect Samples** is not offered.

## B. Pay, then collect

1. **Cashier:** on the patient's visit, **Bill › Create Bill**, submit, and take the payment.
2. **Lab:** press **Refresh** on the worklist and open the row again.
   - [ ] The tests now show *Paid* and are ticked.
3. Press **Collect Samples**.
   - [ ] "3 samples collected". The row leaves *Requests* and appears on *Results Entry*.
   - [ ] Each test's lab test (Open full form › *Sample and Validation*) shows *Sample Collected At* and your name.

## C. Results on one screen

1. On *Results Entry*, open the row and press **Enter Results**.
   - [ ] All three tests are on one screen, each with its normal range.
2. Type an Hb of **8** (below the range).
   - [ ] **L** appears beside it as you type.
3. Fill in the other two and press **Save** (not Validate).
   - [ ] The row moves to *Validate Results*.
4. Open it again, press **Enter Results**, then **Save and Validate** and confirm.
   - [ ] "3 validated". The row is on *Completed*.
   - [ ] Print one lab test (Laboratory Report): it shows the sample collection time and your name as the person who validated.
5. Try validating with a result left empty (repeat A to C with a new patient and leave WBC blank).
   - [ ] WBC is refused with "Please enter the result value"; the other two are validated.

## D. A second person validates (optional)

1. **Administrator:** Kenya Health Settings › Laboratory › tick *A second person validates results*.
2. **Lab:** enter results for a new order and press **Save and Validate**.
   - [ ] Refused: "You entered these results. Another person validates them."
3. A second lab login opens the same order and validates.
   - [ ] Validated.
4. Untick the setting again if the lab doesn't want it.

## E. Walk-in

1. **Lab:** press **Walk-in Request**. Choose a registered patient with no visit today, two tests, *Referred by* "Dr Test, Town Clinic", urgency *Urgent*. Leave *Requesting doctor* blank.
   - [ ] If no default doctor is set in Kenya Health Settings › Laboratory, you are asked to choose one. Choose Dr Amina.
   - [ ] The order opens: two tests, *Urgent*, *Not paid*. The worklist row says *Urgent*.
   - [ ] A new *Lab only* visit was made for the patient, waiting at Laboratory, with the referral recorded.
2. **Cashier:** the visit's **Create Bill** includes the two tests. Bill and take payment.
3. **Lab:** collect, enter and validate as in B and C.
   - [ ] Works the same as a doctor's order.
4. Make a second walk-in request for the same patient the same day.
   - [ ] It goes on the same visit (no second visit).

## F. Results Inbox

1. **Doctor:** open Kenya Health › Laboratory › **Results Inbox**.
   - [ ] It opens on Dr Amina's results. The tests validated in C are there, marked *New*; Hb 8 is marked *Abnormal*.
   - [ ] Reported imaging exams of her patients are listed too (Department *Radiology*).
2. Click one result.
   - [ ] The lab test opens. Back in the inbox it is gone from the list (*Unread only*).
3. Untick *Unread only*.
   - [ ] It is back, with your name and when you opened it under *Seen*.
4. Clear the *Consultant* box.
   - [ ] Every consultant's results are listed.

## G. Who sees what

- [ ] The Lab login sees the Lab Worklist but not the Results Inbox.
- [ ] The Doctor login sees the Results Inbox but not the Lab Worklist.
- [ ] In the walk-in patient search, the Lab login sees phone numbers masked (XXXXXXXX).
