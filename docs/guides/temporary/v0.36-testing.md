# Consultation in Tabs, Pharmacy Substitution Lock and Patient Receipt — Testing Guide

A script to test version 0.36 (0.36.0 and 0.36.1), the consultation and pharmacy part of your outpatient flow feedback of 16 November:
- the consultation is split into tabs in your order: **Observation → Requests → Results → Diagnosis → Prescription → Complete**, with an OBS/Gynae field;
- the doctor sends requests during the consultation, reads the results on the **Results** tab, then completes the consultation and sends the patient on;
- a pharmacist can't swap a prescribed medicine (for example when it is out of stock). The only way is **Send back to the doctor**;
- a **Patient Receipt** for your small 80 mm EPOS printer, with the M-Pesa code. The cashier must type the code for every M-Pesa or card payment;
- (0.36.1) the **consultation fee** is set once, as an item with a price, instead of on every doctor.

Each test says what to do and what the system should do. Tick each one off. If anything behaves differently, send a screenshot.

Test on your own system (**bethesda.royceltd.com**) with your staff logins. The same changes are on the demo hospital (hospital.royceltd.com).

Use new test patients with "Test" in the name, Payment *Cash*.

## A. The consultation tabs

1. Check in a test patient (a woman), take the consultation fee if your hospital charges one, record vitals at triage and send her to the doctor.
2. **Doctor:** open the consultation from your queue.
   - [ ] The tabs read, in order: **Patient**, **Observation**, **Requests**, **Results**, **Diagnosis**, **Prescription**, **Complete**, then *Consultation Details* and *Notes*.
   - [ ] **Patient** has the patient, doctor, date and insurance details.
   - [ ] **Observation** has the presenting complaint, examination and medical history, and an **Obstetric and Gynaecological History** section with an **OBS/Gynae** box. Type something in it and **Save**.
   - [ ] For a **male** patient the OBS/Gynae section is not shown.
   - [ ] Symptoms, appointment type and source are still not on the form.

## B. Requests and results

1. **Doctor:** on **Requests**, add a lab test (for example *Full Haemogram*). Click **Send Requests**.
   - [ ] A message says the requests were sent. The consultation stays open (still a draft).
   - [ ] A box asks **Where does the patient go next?** and suggests the **Cashier** (the test is paid first). Choose *Cashier* and **Send**.
2. **Doctor:** open **Results**.
   - [ ] The test is listed: **Awaiting payment at the Cashier**.
3. **Cashier:** bill the test and take the payment (see part D for the receipt).
4. **Doctor:** reload the consultation, **Results**.
   - [ ] The test now says **Waiting at the lab**.
5. **Lab:** collect the sample, enter the results and validate them on the Lab Worklist.
   - [ ] **Doctor:** **Results** shows the status as it moves: *Sample collected*, *Results entered, awaiting validation*, then **Validated** with each result, its unit and normal range. Abnormal results are in **red**.
   - [ ] **Mark Seen** on the result changes to *Seen*. The result also leaves the doctor's Results Inbox (unread).
6. An X-ray or ultrasound ordered the same way shows on **Results** with its findings and impression once Radiology reports it.

## C. Diagnosis, prescription and completing the consultation

1. **Doctor:** **Diagnosis** tab: add the diagnosis.
2. **Prescription** tab: prescribe a medicine. **Save**.
3. **Complete** tab: review notes and **Referral** (tick *Referred to Another Facility* to see *Referred To*).
   - [ ] The **Referral** fields are visible again. Version 0.34 had hidden them by mistake, together with *Source*.
4. Click **Complete Consultation** (on the Complete tab, or the blue button at the top).
   - [ ] It asks you to confirm, then the consultation is submitted (no longer editable).
   - [ ] **Where does the patient go next?** suggests **Pharmacy**. The list also has *Finish visit (patient goes home)*. Choose *Pharmacy* and **Send**.
   - [ ] **Pharmacist:** the patient is on the Pharmacy Worklist.

## D. The pharmacist can't substitute a medicine

1. **Pharmacist:** on the Pharmacy Worklist, **Bill Drugs** for the patient. Open the draft bill (Sales Invoice) and change the prescribed medicine on its line to a different one. **Save**.
   - [ ] Refused: *"… was prescribed by Dr …, not …. A prescribed medicine is not changed on the bill or at dispensing … use Send back to the doctor"*.
   - [ ] The same happens on a Delivery Note for the prescription.
   - [ ] Counter sales to walk-in customers (no prescription) are not affected.
2. **Pharmacist:** for a medicine that is out of stock, use **Send back to the doctor** with a note (for example "Out of stock, please prescribe an alternative"). The medicine must not be on a bill yet: the Cashier removes it from the draft bill first.
   - [ ] The patient goes back to the doctor's queue.
   - [ ] **Doctor:** the consultation shows an orange note at the top, *"Pharmacy sent back …"*, and the **Prescription** tab lists the medicine with the pharmacist's note.

## E. Patient receipt and M-Pesa code

1. **Cashier:** take a payment on a patient's invoice by **M-Pesa** without typing the code. **Submit**.
   - [ ] Refused: *"Enter the transaction code (e.g. the M-Pesa code) …"*.
   - [ ] The code goes in the **Reference No** column of the payments table. Type it and submit.
   - [ ] A **Cash** payment needs no code.
   - [ ] The same applies to a payment made with **Payment Entry**.
2. On the paid invoice, click **Patient Receipt**.
   - [ ] A receipt opens sized for the 80 mm printer, with: the hospital's logo, name, contacts and KRA PIN; the invoice number and date; the patient's name, number, date of birth, gender and phone; the payment mode and **M-Pesa code**; who paid; the items with quantity, unit cost and amount; discount, net total, the amount tendered and change; and **Served by** with the cashier's name.
   - [ ] Print it on the EPOS printer. In the print dialog, choose the receipt printer, paper size 80 mm, margins *None*.

Tell us anything on the receipt to add, remove or move: we will match your current slip.

## F. Setting the consultation fee (version 0.36.1)

The consultation fee is now an item with a price on the price list. One fee for everyone is set in one place; a doctor or a clinic with a different fee gets its own item.

**Manager, once:**
1. Stock › Item: check there is a service item **General Consultation** (not a stock item, VAT exempt). Create it if not.
2. Stock › Item Price: give it the price **200** on the *Standard Selling* price list.
3. Kenya Health › Kenya Health Settings › Payment Before Service: **Default Consultation Item** = *General Consultation*. Save.
4. On each doctor (Healthcare › Practitioner › Charges), leave *Out Patient Consulting Charge Item* **empty** unless that doctor charges a different fee (then the item and its amount are both filled in).

Tests:
- [ ] **Reception:** check in a cash outpatient with any doctor. The visit waits at the **Cashier**; **Bill** shows the consultation at **200**.
- [ ] Change the Item Price to 250 and check in another test patient: the fee is **250**. (Set it back to 200.)
- [ ] **A doctor with a different fee:** create a service item *Specialist Consultation*. On that doctor, set *Out Patient Consulting Charge Item* = *Specialist Consultation* and *Out Patient Consulting Charge* = **1,000**. A patient checked in to that doctor pays **1,000**; other doctors stay at 200.
- [ ] **Free review:** create *Review Consultation* priced **0** and add it to the *Review* Service Type (Healthcare › Appointment Type › Review › Billing). A *Review* patient on that service goes straight to triage, without a stop at the Cashier.
- [ ] **No fee set up:** clear the Default Consultation Item and the doctor's item, then check in a cash outpatient. Reception sees **No consultation fee** with what is missing, and the patient goes to triage. (Set the default back.)
- [ ] **Insured patient:** the consultation goes on the insurer's bill at the scheme's price for *General Consultation* (or the cash price if the scheme has none).
- [ ] **Patient Receipt:** print a receipt with a fee of 1,500 or more. The *Qty*, *Unit Cost* and *Amount* columns are separate and readable (0.36.0 ran them together).
