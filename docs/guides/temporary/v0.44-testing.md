# Changes from the 17 and 18 November Demos — Testing Guide

This is a short testing script for version 0.44.1. It covers every point raised in the 17 and 18 November demos that this version changes. The points already handled in versions 0.41 and 0.42 (pay before triage, the queues, price books and categories) are in the guide *Your Feedback of 17 November*.

Test on the demo, **hospital.royceltd.com**. Sign in with the role logins (Reception, Cashier, Doctor, Lab), not as Administrator. Administrator skips some checks on purpose, so it doesn't show what each department sees. Tick each item as you go, and send a screenshot if anything is different.

## A. Registration (Reception)

1. Patient › **+ Add**. Choose a **County**, then a **Sub-County**, then a **Residence**.
   - [ ] The county and sub-county you chose stay as they are. Before this fix, the residence cleared them.
2. Choose a residence before choosing a county.
   - [ ] If that residence has a county saved, the county fills in by itself.
3. Look at **Invite as User**.
   - [ ] It is unticked.
4. Save.
   - [ ] The patient is saved, and no "Please Update SMS Settings" message appears.

## B. Observation before requests (Doctor)

Use a paid cash patient who is waiting in the Doctor's Queue.

1. Open a consultation. Leave the **Observation** tab empty, add a lab test on the **Requests** tab, and press **Send Requests**.
   - [ ] Refused with "Record the observation before requesting tests or prescribing", followed by a list of the empty fields.
2. Fill in **Chief Complaint**, **History of Presenting Illness** and **Physical Examination**, then press **Send Requests** again.
   - [ ] The requests are sent, and the "Where does the patient go next?" box opens.
3. Do the same for a **Casualty** visit.
   - [ ] The requests are sent even though the Observation tab is empty.

**Manager:** Kenya Health Settings › **Consultation** sets which fields are required, and can switch the rule off.

## C. The right column for each request (Doctor)

1. On the **Requests** tab, look at the lab requests table.
   - [ ] It has a **Lab Test** column and an **Imaging** column.
2. Click in the **Imaging** column.
   - [ ] Only imaging exams are offered (X-ray, ultrasound). Lab tests and vital signs are not.
3. Order a *Full Haemogram* in the **Lab Test** column, send it, and have the Cashier take payment.
   - [ ] It appears on the **Lab Worklist**.

**Administrator:** set up lab tests as **Lab Test Templates**. **Observation Templates** are only for imaging, with the category *Imaging*. The *FULL HAEMOGRAM* created during the demo is an Observation Template, so it is refused when ordered. Create it as a Lab Test Template instead (see *Your Feedback of 17 November*, part C2).

## D. A request with no price (Doctor, then Administrator)

1. **Administrator:** pick a lab test and delete its price on the cash price list (Stock › Item Price).
2. **Doctor:** request that test and press **Send Requests**.
   - [ ] The "Where does the patient go next?" box shows an orange warning that names the test. It says the Cashier can't bill the test until its price is set.
3. **Administrator:** put the price back.

## E. Requests not sent yet (Doctor)

1. Add a lab test on the **Requests** tab and press **Save**, not Send Requests.
   - [ ] An orange note at the top says "1 request(s) not sent yet: click Send Requests…".

## F. Triage without extra clicks (Nurse)

Use a paid cash patient who is waiting at Triage.

1. Kenya Health › Triage › **Triage Queue**: click the patient.
   - [ ] Their **Vital Signs** form opens, already linked to the visit.
2. Record the readings and **Save**.
   - [ ] On the Triage Queue, the patient shows *Triage: seen*. You didn't press Seen.
3. **Submit** the vital signs.
   - [ ] A message says who the patient was sent to (the doctor and room). They leave the Triage Queue and appear in the **Doctor's Queue**.
4. Outside every doctor's Practitioner Schedule (for example at night), submit another patient's vital signs.
   - [ ] An orange message says no doctor is on duty. The patient is in the **Doctor's Queue** with no doctor, and the first doctor who opens their consultation takes them.

## G. The doctor's queue (Doctor)

1. Kenya Health › Consultation › **Doctor's Queue** › **My Patients**: click the patient.
   - [ ] Their **Consultation** opens, filled from the visit. If you had already started one, that one opens.
   - [ ] **Latest Vital Signs** shows at the top of every tab. Abnormal readings are in red.

## H. The bill is ready for the Cashier (Reception, Doctor, Cashier)

1. **Reception:** check in a cash patient (*Outpatient*).
   - [ ] The visit's bill already lists a **Draft** invoice with the consultation fee.
2. **Cashier:** open the patient from the **Cashier Queue** and press **Bill › Take Payment**.
   - [ ] It pays the draft. You don't need to press Create Bill.
3. **Doctor:** request two lab tests, and press **Send Requests**.
   - [ ] Back on the visit, both tests are on a new draft bill.
4. **Cashier:** open the draft, delete one test line, save, then **Take Payment**.
   - [ ] Only the remaining test is paid. The deleted one stays under *Not billed yet* on the visit and is not added back to the bill.

## I. M-Pesa paid more, or less (Cashier)

1. **Take Payment** with **M-Pesa** for 200 more than the bill, with a transaction code.
   - [ ] The message says *Kept as the patient's credit: 200*.
2. At the same patient's next bill, press **Take Payment**.
   - [ ] **Use the patient's credit of 200** is ticked, and *Left to pay* is 200 less than the bill.
3. **Take Payment** with M-Pesa for less than the bill.
   - [ ] *Part payment*: the rest stays owed, and the lab and pharmacy wait until it is paid.
4. With M-Pesa prompts set up: press **Send M-Pesa Prompt**, check the patient's number, and **Send**.
   - [ ] The phone shows the M-Pesa prompt. Once the PIN is entered, the box says *Payment taken*.

## J. Each role sees its own work (Administrator, then each role)

1. **Administrator:** Kenya Health › Setup › **Compliance Check**.
   - [ ] The *Access* row passes: no role has more access than the role matrix gives it.
2. **Doctor**, **Nurse** and **Lab**: open a patient's registration record.
   - [ ] It is read-only. Only Reception and the Healthcare Administrator edit it.
3. **Reception** and **Cashier**: look for Consultations, Lab Tests and the MOH 705 report.
   - [ ] None of them open.
4. **Lab**, **Pharmacy** and **Cashier**: open a patient's visit.
   - [ ] **Record › Vital Signs / Consultation** and **Cancel Visit** don't show. Each role sees only the buttons for its own work.
5. **Reception:** check in a patient and look at the visit form.
   - [ ] Company, facility, date, doctor and room are under **More Details**, closed and already filled in.

## K. Requests go when the doctor saves (Doctor)

1. On a consultation with the Observation tab filled, add a lab test on the **Requests** tab and **Save**.
   - [ ] The test is sent: it shows on the **Results** tab as waiting, and a cash patient's test is on the visit's draft bill.
2. Add a second test and **Save** again.
   - [ ] The second test is sent too, and the consultation stays open.
3. Delete the row of a test that the lab hasn't started, and **Save**.
   - [ ] That test is cancelled. It leaves the Lab Worklist and the draft bill.
4. On a new consultation with the Observation tab empty, add a test and **Save**.
   - [ ] The consultation is saved, and an orange note says the requests weren't sent. Fill the observation and save again: they go.

## L. Completing the consultation (Doctor)

1. Look at the top of the consultation.
   - [ ] There's one main button, **Complete Consultation**, and no **Submit**.
2. Prescribe a medicine before entering a diagnosis, and **Save**.
   - [ ] Refused: enter the diagnosis first. A provisional one is fine.
3. With a lab test still waiting for its result, press **Complete Consultation**.
   - [ ] Refused, naming the test that has no result yet.
4. On the **Results** tab, press **Cancel Request** on that test, give a reason, then complete.
   - [ ] The test shows *Cancelled* with the reason, and the consultation completes.

## M. The visit closes by itself (Reception, Cashier, Pharmacy)

1. Take a cash patient through the whole visit: check-in, fee, triage, consultation with a test, pay, result, complete the consultation, pay for the medicine, dispense.
   - [ ] After the last step, the visit shows **Completed** on its own.
2. On another patient's open visit, press **Status › Complete Visit** while something is still open (for example a test without a result).
   - [ ] **Reception:** refused, with a list of what is still open.
   - [ ] **Healthcare Administrator:** asked for a reason, then the visit closes. The reason is kept on the visit.
3. **Status › Cancel Visit** on a visit where the doctor has started a consultation.
   - [ ] Refused: work has started on it, so complete it instead. On a visit where nothing has happened yet, it cancels.
4. **Administrator:** Kenya Health › Patients › **Visits to Close**.
   - [ ] It lists visits open for more than a day, with what each is waiting for. Choose *Completed with open items* to see visits that were closed with something still open.
5. On a patient's record, press **Close Visit**.
   - [ ] It works the same way as Complete Visit.

## N. Cashier Queue and receipt (Cashier)

1. Kenya Health › Billing › **Cashier Queue**: click a patient.
   - [ ] Their bill opens, with **Take Payment** and **Open Visit**.
2. Print the **Patient Receipt** for an invoice that KRA has signed.
   - [ ] The receipt shows **KRA eTIMS**: the PIN, CU invoice number, receipt number, signature and QR code.

## O. Patient file, registration and screen lock

1. **Doctor or Nurse:** on a patient's record, press **View › Patient File**.
   - [ ] The whole record opens: history, admissions, then every visit with vital signs, consultations, diagnoses, medicines, lab results and imaging reports. It prints.
   - [ ] **Reception** and the **Cashier** don't see the button. If they open the page directly, it says the file is for clinicians only.
2. **Reception:** start a new patient and enter a date of birth.
   - [ ] For an adult, **Mobile** is marked with an asterisk. For a child, **Next of Kin Phone** is.
3. **Reception:** open **Patient Visit › + Add** right after looking at another patient's visit.
   - [ ] The new visit shows no invoices.
4. Let the screen lock, then reload the page.
   - [ ] It is still locked. **Log out** on the lock screen logs out at once.

## P. Pharmacy (Pharmacist)

1. **Counter Sale**: type in the Drug field.
   - [ ] Only drugs are offered. Other stock items, such as building materials, are not.
2. On a patient with two consultations, press **Print Prescription**.
   - [ ] There's one button. It asks which consultation, listed by time and doctor.
