# The Order Drug Panel — Testing Guide

This is a short testing script for version 0.50.0. It covers the prescription screen Bethesda asked for (page 4 of the requirements document): ordering drugs one at a time or as a package, with the pharmacy's stock, and the patient's pending, sent, active, past and chronic medicines side by side.

Test on the demo, **hospital.royceltd.com**, or on your local server. Use a Doctor login, and a Pharmacist login for part E. Tick each item as you go, and send a screenshot if anything is different.

## Before you start

- **A patient:** use one who has paid the consultation fee and is in the Doctor's Queue.
- **A consultation:** open it and write the Chief Complaint, History of Presenting Illness, Physical Examination and a provisional diagnosis.
- **Part C needs a drug package.** In Healthcare Setup, open **Treatment Plan Template**, make *Malaria (adult)* and add two drugs to its **Drugs** table, for example an antimalarial and paracetamol.

## A. One drug

1. Open the **Prescription** tab.
   - [ ] **Order Drug** is on the left, with **Single Drug** selected. **Pending, Sent, Active, Past, Chronic** are on the right.
2. **Drug Class:** choose one of the pharmacy's groups, then type in **Drug Name**.
   - [ ] Only drugs of that class are offered, each with *Available: N*.
3. Choose a drug in stock.
   - [ ] A green **Available** badge shows its stock. The unit and route fill in when the drug has them set.
4. **Dose** *1*, unit *Tablet*, **Frequency** *TID*, **Duration** *5 Day*, **Route** *Oral*.
   - [ ] *To dispense: 15 …* shows under the form.
5. **Add to Pending**.
   - [ ] The drug is on the **Pending** tab with a grey **Draft** label. The form clears, keeping the class and start.
6. Tick **Schedule dosage?**, put *Every 8 Hour*, and add a second drug.
   - [ ] *every 8 Hour* shows on its pending row.

## B. Save to Draft, then Submit

1. **Save to Draft**.
   - [ ] *Saved. Draft drugs are not sent yet.* The Pharmacy Worklist does not show the drugs.
2. Click **Complete Consultation**.
   - [ ] It is refused: *These drugs are still in draft: …*.
3. Back on the Prescription tab, **Submit**.
   - [ ] *2 drug(s) sent to the pharmacy*. The **Sent** tab lists both as *Waiting*. The Pharmacy Worklist shows them.

## C. A drug package

1. Click **Drug Package**, choose *Malaria (adult)*, then **Add Package to Pending**.
   - [ ] Both drugs of the package are on **Pending**, with the package's dose and frequency.
2. Remove one with **Remove**, then **Submit**.
   - [ ] Only the other one is sent.

## D. Buy externally, chronic, and the lists

1. Add a drug with **Buy Externally** ticked, and another with **Chronic medicine** ticked. **Submit**.
   - [ ] On **Sent**, the first shows *Buy Outside*. It isn't billed and the pharmacy doesn't dispense it.
2. **Complete Consultation**. Start a new consultation for the same patient on another visit.
   - [ ] **Active** lists the earlier drugs still within their course.
   - [ ] **Chronic** lists the chronic one, with **Continue**.
3. **Continue** on the chronic drug.
   - [ ] The form fills with its dose, frequency, duration and route. Check it, then **Add to Pending**.
4. When a course has ended (or on a patient with older prescriptions):
   - [ ] It shows under **Past**, with **Prescribe Again**.

## E. Pharmacy (Pharmacist)

1. Open the patient on the **Pharmacy Worklist**.
   - [ ] Each drug shows the dose, frequency, duration and route as the doctor wrote them, and the quantity from the panel.
   - [ ] The *Buy Externally* drug is under *Buy Outside*, not *Waiting*.
