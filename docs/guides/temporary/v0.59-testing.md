# Version 0.59: the Casualty Module — Testing Guide

This testing script covers version 0.59.0, the Casualty module from your requirements (points 6 and 7):

- **Send to Casualty from triage.** The patient appears on a new **Casualty** list.
- **A casualty bed.** Giving the patient a bed opens their screen with the casualty tabs: Patient File, Vitals, TB Screening, Diagnosis, Nursing Notes, Nursing Charts and Care Plan, Doctor Notes, Doctor Instructions, Requests, Results, Billing, **Casualty Drugs**, Disposition and Discharge.
- **From casualty to a ward** (Admit to Ward), or **home** (Discharge).
- **Charging.** The casualty bed is charged as you set it: per hour, per day, or not at all.
- **Hospital statistics.** A casualty stay is not counted as an admission (MOH 717, bed occupancy) unless the patient is admitted to a ward.

Test on the demo, **hospital.royceltd.com**. Press **Ctrl+Shift+R** on each page first, so your browser loads the new version. Tick each item as you go, and send a screenshot if anything is different.

## Before you start

The demo has a **Casualty** unit with 3 beds (CAS Bed 01 to 03), charged **KES 300 per hour**. You need a patient checked in for an outpatient visit and waiting at **Triage**.

## A. From triage to casualty (Triage Nurse)

1. Open the patient's visit from the Triage queue and record the vital signs as usual.
2. On the visit, press **Send To**. Choose Station **Casualty**, write a note (for example *Road traffic accident, head injury*), and press **Send**.
   - [ ] The visit shows the patient at **Casualty**.
3. In the sidebar, open **Casualty** (under Ward).
   - [ ] The patient is under **Sent to Casualty** with the urgency, the Casualty unit and your note as the reason.
   - [ ] The patient is **not** on the **Ward** page: casualty patients have their own list.

A patient checked in with visit type **Casualty** (for example, brought by ambulance) goes straight to this list, without triage.

## B. A casualty bed and the casualty screen (Casualty Nurse)

1. On the Casualty list, press **Give Bed**, choose *CAS Bed 01* and press **Admit**.
   - [ ] The patient's screen opens with the casualty tabs listed above, **Casualty Drugs** among them.
   - [ ] The button at the top right says **Casualty** and takes you back to the Casualty list.
   - [ ] On the Casualty list, the patient is under **In Casualty** with the bed and the time they arrived.
2. Record vital signs, a nursing note and a chart entry (Nursing Charts and Care Plan), as on the ward.
   - [ ] Each one shows in its tab's table.

## C. The doctor in casualty (Doctor)

1. Open the patient from the Casualty list.
2. On **Diagnosis**, add a diagnosis with its ICD-11 code.
3. On **Requests**, send a lab test or an X-ray.
   - [ ] It shows in the tab's table and reaches the lab or radiology.
4. On **Casualty Drugs** › **Prescription**, prescribe a drug and press **Save Prescription**.
   - [ ] The pharmacy sees it. Once sent to the bed, the nurse gives it from **Administration Log** › **Give Dose**.
5. On **Billing**:
   - [ ] the running bill shows the casualty bed by the hour, plus the tests and drugs.

## D. Leaving casualty

### D1. Admitted to a ward

1. On **Disposition**, press **Admit to Ward**. Choose the **General Ward**, give the reason and the urgency, and send.
2. Open **Ward**, choose the General Ward.
   - [ ] The patient is under **Incoming transfers**. Press **Receive** and give a bed.
   - [ ] The patient is now on the ward, and their screen has the ward's tabs.
   - [ ] They have left the Casualty list.
   - [ ] They count as an admission from now on.

### D2. Home from casualty

1. With another casualty patient, use **Disposition** and **Discharge** as on the ward.
   - [ ] The patient leaves the Casualty list. Their bill has the hours in casualty.
   - [ ] **MOH 717** for the month does not count them as an admission or a discharge.

## Setting up casualty at your hospital (Administrator)

Casualty is set up as data, so you decide how it works. Nothing changes at your hospital until you do these steps.

1. **The unit.** Open **Healthcare Service Unit**, open (or make) your casualty unit as a group. Tick **Casualty Unit**.
2. **The beds.** Add the casualty beds under it. Each needs a **Service Unit Type** with **Inpatient Occupancy**.
3. **The charge.** Set it on the beds' **Service Unit Type**:
   - per hour: **No of Hours** 1, with the hourly rate;
   - per day: **No of Hours** 24, with the daily rate;
   - not charged: untick **Is Billable**.
4. **The tabs.** In **Patient Workspace Profile** › **Casualty**, you can rename, reorder or hide tabs. **Kenya Health Settings** › **Casualty Patient Screen Tabs** chooses which profile casualty uses.
