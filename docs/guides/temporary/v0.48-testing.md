# The Ward Screen as at Bethesda: Charts, TB Screening, Doctor's Notes, Consent and Disposition — Testing Guide

This is a short testing script for version 0.48.0. It brings the inpatient screen of version 0.47 closer to the ward screen Bethesda uses today (their document of 19 November):

- their nursing charts, including scored charts (Glasgow Coma Scale, Thompson HIE score);
- a TB Screening tab;
- doctor's notes and instructions with the patient's status;
- Consent and Disposition tabs;
- a patient report where you choose what to include.

Test on the demo, **hospital.royceltd.com**, or on your local server. Use the Doctor and Nurse logins. Admit a patient first (v0.47 guide, parts B and C). Tick each item as you go, and send a screenshot if anything is different.

## A. The tabs (Nurse)

1. Kenya Health › Inpatient › **Ward** › open a patient.
   - [ ] The tabs read: Patient File, Vitals, TB Screening, Diagnosis, Doctor Notes, Doctor Instructions, Nursing Notes, Nursing Care, Drug Administration, Requests, Results, Consent, Billing, Disposition, Discharge.
2. **Administrator:** Patient Workspace Profile › *Inpatient*: rename a tab or tick **Hidden** on one, save, reload the patient screen.
   - [ ] The change shows at once. Put it back after.

## B. Nursing charts (Nurse)

1. **Nursing Care** tab.
   - [ ] Besides Feeding, Blood Transfusion and Hygiene and Turning, these charts are there: Glasgow Coma Scale, Paediatric Glasgow Coma Scale, Thompson HIE Score, NBU Observation Chart, Post Operation Chart, Dressing Chart, Diabetic Chart, Oxygen Administration and Fetal Kick Chart.
   - [ ] Observation (vital signs) is on the **Vitals** tab, fluid balance under **Intake / Output**, and the partograph is the Labour Care Guide in Maternity.
2. Glasgow Coma Scale › **Add Entry**: eye *2 To pain*, verbal *2 Incomprehensible sounds*, motor *4 Withdraws from pain*.
   - [ ] The **GCS** column shows **8**, flagged in red (8 or less).
3. Another entry with *4*, *5*, *6*.
   - [ ] GCS **15**, not flagged.
4. Diabetic Chart with blood glucose *3.2*.
   - [ ] Shown in red (below 4).
5. **Administrator:** open Nursing Chart Template › *Diabetic Chart*: change the alert range or add a column, save.
   - [ ] The next entry uses it. Any new chart can be added the same way, without a new version.

## C. TB screening (Nurse)

1. **TB Screening** tab › **Add Entry**: cough *Yes*, the rest *No*, TB status *Presumed TB*.
   - [ ] Cough shows in red. The entry is listed with the time, shift and nurse.
2. **Nursing Care** tab.
   - [ ] TB Screening is not repeated there.

## D. Doctor's notes and instructions (Doctor, Nurse)

1. **Doctor:** **Doctor Notes** tab › **Add Doctor's Note**: *Afebrile, eating well*, patient status *Improving*.
   - [ ] Listed with the time, shift, doctor and a green *Improving*.
2. **Doctor Instructions** tab › **Add Instructions for the Nurses**: *Encourage oral fluids, 2 L a day*.
   - [ ] Listed there, not under Doctor Notes.
3. **Nurse:** **Nursing Notes** tab › **Note with Patient Status**: *Slept well*, *Stable*.
   - [ ] Listed with *Stable*. The doctor's notes and instructions are not mixed in.
4. **Nurse:** try **Add Doctor's Note**.
   - [ ] Refused: doctor's notes and instructions are written by doctors.

## E. Consent (Doctor)

1. **Consent** tab › **New Consent**.
   - [ ] The consent form opens for this patient (procedure, what was explained, who consented, witness, signed form).
2. Save and sign it, then return to the tab.
   - [ ] Listed with the procedure, date, who gave consent and *Signed*.

## F. Disposition (Doctor)

1. **Disposition** tab › **Set Disposition**: *Referred Out* with no facility.
   - [ ] Refused: say which facility.
2. *Referred Out* to *Moi Teaching and Referral Hospital*, note *For CT scan*.
   - [ ] Shown on the tab. Print the discharge summary (Discharge tab): **Outcome: Referred Out: Moi Teaching and Referral Hospital**.
   - [ ] MOH 717 counts the admission as a referral out once discharged.

## G. Patient report (Doctor or Nurse)

1. **Patient File** tab › **Patient Report (choose sections)**.
   - [ ] A box lists Medical history, Admissions, Vital signs, Consultations (diagnoses and medicines), Requests, Lab results and Imaging reports, all ticked.
2. Untick everything except **Vital signs** and **Lab results**, **Open**.
   - [ ] The report shows only the vital signs and lab results of each visit.

## H. Discharge summary (Doctor)

1. Print the discharge summary of an admission with doctor's notes.
   - [ ] A **Ward rounds** section lists the doctor's notes with their status, and **Outcome** shows the disposition.
