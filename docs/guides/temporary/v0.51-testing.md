# Your Feedback of 20 November, Part 1: Quick Fixes — Testing Guide

This is a short testing script for version 0.51.0. It covers the first part of the feedback of 20 November: the screens running into the sidebar, triage, the consultation's Observation tab, the blood transfusion chart, the WHO Time Out, the new OPD report by payment mode, and diagnosis search by ICD-11 name or code.

Test on the demo, **hospital.royceltd.com**. Use the logins named in each part. Tick each item as you go, and send a screenshot if anything is different.

## A. Screens off the sidebar (any login)

1. Open the **Lab Worklist**, the **Pharmacy Worklist**, the **Ward** and an admitted patient's screen.
   - [ ] On each one, the content starts a little way from the sidebar instead of touching it.
   - [ ] A wide table scrolls left and right inside the page, and nothing runs under the sidebar.

## B. Triage (Nurse)

1. Open **Triage Queue** and record vital signs for a patient.
   - [ ] **Tongue**, **Abdomen** and **Reflexes** are not on the form.
   - [ ] Temperature, pulse, respiratory rate, blood pressure, SpO2, height and weight are still there.

## C. The Observation tab (Doctor)

1. Open a new consultation for a woman patient and go to **Observation**.
   - [ ] There is one form with no headings and no *"From the patient's record…"* note.
   - [ ] The fields are side by side in this order:
     - **Chief Complaint** | **History of Presenting Illness**
     - **Review of Systems** | **Past Medical History**, **Past Surgical History**
     - **Family History** | **Social History**
     - **OBS/Gynae**
     - **Physical Examination**, last.
   - [ ] Allergies and Current Medication are not on this tab. They are still on the patient's record.
2. Open a consultation for a man.
   - [ ] **OBS/Gynae** is not shown.

## D. Diagnosis by name or code (Doctor)

1. On the **Diagnosis** tab, add a row and type *malaria* in **Diagnosis**.
   - [ ] ICD-11 matches are offered. Choosing one fills the **ICD-11 Code**.
2. Add another row and type a code, for example *1F40*, in **ICD-11 Code**.
   - [ ] The **Diagnosis** fills with its name.

Both sites now have the full ICD-11 list, 35,664 codes (WHO release 2026-01).

## E. Blood transfusion (Nurse)

1. Open an admitted patient › **Nursing Charts** › **Blood Transfusion**.
   - [ ] The heading says *Counter-checked by a second nurse* in grey. There is no orange label.
2. **Add Entry**.
   - [ ] **Counter Checked By** is on the form, with the note that a second nurse checks the patient and the unit.
3. Fill the readings, leave **Counter Checked By** empty and save.
   - [ ] The form asks for **Counter Checked By**.
4. Choose yourself and save.
   - [ ] *Counter Checked By must be another nurse, not you.*
5. Choose another nurse and save.
   - [ ] The entry is saved.

## F. WHO Time Out (Theatre nurse)

1. Open a theatre booking's **Surgical Safety Checklist**, complete **Sign In**, then go to **Time Out**.
   - [ ] **Surgeon:** *Procedure Steps* (Critical, Non-routine or Routine), *Expected Duration* and *Anticipated Blood Loss* are separate fields.
   - [ ] **Anaesthetist:** *Patient-Specific Concerns*, Yes or No. A **Yes** opens a box for the concerns.
   - [ ] **Nursing:** *Sterility Confirmed*, Yes or No, and *Equipment Issues or Concerns*, Yes or No. A **Yes** opens a box for the details.
2. Answer **Yes** to equipment issues, leave the box empty, enter who led the Time Out and save.
   - [ ] It asks for the details.
3. Write the details and save.
   - [ ] Time Out shows *Done*, with the time.
4. **Print › Theatre Checklist**.
   - [ ] Each Time Out answer is printed on its own line.

## G. OPD patients by payment mode (Reception, Cashier or Administrator)

1. Kenya Health › Reports › **OPD Patients by Payment Mode**.
   - [ ] Today's visits are counted per payment mode: *Cash* first, then each scheme (for example *SHA - PHC*, *SHA - MWALIMU*) or insurer, with a total row and a bar chart.
   - [ ] **Patients** counts each patient once, even with two visits.
2. Change **From Date** and **To Date** to a week, and **Visit Type** to *Outpatient*.
   - [ ] The counts change to match.
