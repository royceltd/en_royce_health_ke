# Versions 0.54 to 0.57: Partograph, Theatre, Patient Flow, MOH Reports and Imaging — Testing Guide

This testing script covers everything released from version 0.54 to version 0.57.0, the rest of your feedback of 20 November and the monthly MOH reports:

- **The Partograph** (pages 5 to 7 of your feedback): your 7-section form on the ward's Nursing Charts.
- **The Theatre List tabs:** select a patient on the Theatre List and their screen opens with **Theatre Doctor Notes** and **Drug Administration**.
- **The patient's way through the hospital** (OPD → Admission → Ward → Theatre or Maternity). Each hand-over arrives as a request in the next place:
  - procedures are sent to **Theatre Requests** and booked from there;
  - a patient is **transferred between wards**, for example to the Maternity Ward.
- **MOH reports from your monthly list:**
  - the commodity reports (F-CDRR: MOH 747A, 730A/730B, 643B, TB and leprosy);
  - the vaccine fridge's temperature sheet;
  - the immunisation register and **MOH 710**.
- **Imaging:** **View Images** opens an exam's pictures in your image viewer, from its accession number.

The parts follow the patient: consultation and ward, theatre, maternity, then reports. Test on the demo, **hospital.royceltd.com**. Use the login each part names. Tick each item as you go, and send a screenshot if anything is different.

Still open:
- **HIV reports (MOH 729B, HTS tally):** tell us whether your comprehensive care clinic uses KenyaEMR (see part F).
- **Radiology:** tell us what is still pending there (see part G).

## Before you start

- **For parts A and C:** one admitted patient on the **General Ward**, and one woman admitted to a ward.
- **For part B:** an admitted patient with a theatre booking for today. Part C1 makes one from a request.
- The demo has a second ward, the **Maternity Ward** (2 beds), and the procedure *Appendicectomy (open)* is set up to go to Theatre.

## A. Sending a patient to Theatre (Doctor, then Theatre)

### A1. From the ward

1. On the admitted patient's screen, open **Requests**.
2. Choose Request Type **Theatre**, the service *Appendicectomy (open)*, urgency **Urgent**, a note (for example *Acute abdomen*), and send.
   - [ ] The request shows in the tab's list.
3. Open **Theatre › Theatre Requests**.
   - [ ] The patient is listed with **Urgent** in red, **From** (ward and bed), the procedure, who asked and the doctor's note.
   - [ ] Emergencies come first, then urgent, then elective.
4. Press **Book**.
   - [ ] A new Theatre Booking opens with the patient, procedure, priority, admission and request filled in.
   - [ ] Fill in the theatre, time and surgeon, then save.
   - [ ] The request leaves Theatre Requests.
   - [ ] On the **Ward**, the patient's row shows **Theatre** and the time.
5. On the booking, press **Cancel** and give a reason.
   - [ ] The request is back on Theatre Requests.

### A2. From the consultation (outpatient)

1. In an outpatient consultation, add *Appendicectomy (open)* on the Requests tab (type **Theatre**) and press **Send Requests**.
   - [ ] **Theatre** is offered as where the patient goes next.
   - [ ] The request is on Theatre Requests with **From**: *Outpatient*.
   - [ ] A patient sent to Theatre keeps the visit open; it does not close by itself.

## B. The Theatre List tabs (Theatre Doctor)

1. Open **Theatre › Theatre List** for today.
   - [ ] The patient's name is a link. A patient who is not admitted shows *(not admitted)*, and the link opens the booking instead.
2. Select the admitted patient.
   - [ ] Their screen opens with the theatre's tabs: **Theatre Doctor Notes**, **Drug Administration**, **Vitals**, **Results**, **Patient File**.
   - [ ] The banner has a **Theatre** line: procedure, theatre, time, surgeon, status and the booking number. The booking number opens the booking.
   - [ ] The button at the top right says **Theatre List** and takes you back.

### B1. Theatre Doctor Notes

1. On **Theatre Doctor Notes**, fill in **Procedure Notes** and **Complications**, leave **Date and Time** and **By (Doctor)** as they are, and press **Save Note**.
   - [ ] **By (Doctor)** started as you, or as the booking's surgeon.
   - [ ] The note is in the table, newest first, with the complications in red and who entered it.
   - [ ] Save a second note with no complications. Its row says *None*.
2. Open the booking and look at **Continuation Notes**.
   - [ ] The same notes are there. A saved note can't be changed; a correction is a new note.

### B2. Drug Administration

1. Open **Drug Administration**.
   - [ ] It is the ward's drug tab: **Prescription** (the drug screen), **Dispensing** and **Administration Log**.
   - [ ] A drug prescribed here appears on the ward's Drug Administration tab for the same admission, and the other way round.

## C. Transfer to another ward, for example Maternity (Nurse)

1. On the woman's patient screen (General Ward), open **Disposition** and press **Transfer to Ward**.
2. Choose **Maternity Ward**, urgency **Urgent**, a reason (*In labour*), and press **Send**.
   - [ ] A blue note in the banner says the transfer is asked; the patient stays where she is until a bed is given.
   - [ ] On the **Ward** page (General Ward), her row shows **Transfer to Maternity Ward asked**.
3. On the **Ward** page, choose **Maternity Ward**.
   - [ ] She is under **Incoming transfers**, with urgency, where she is now and the reason.
4. Press **Receive**, choose a bed, and confirm.
   - [ ] Her screen opens with **Maternity Ward** and the new bed in the banner.
   - [ ] Her old bed on the General Ward is free again.
5. Ask a transfer for another patient, then press **Cancel Transfer** on the Disposition tab.
   - [ ] The patient leaves the other ward's Incoming transfers.

## D. The Partograph (Nurse or Midwife)

### D1. Where it is

1. On the woman's screen, open **Nursing Care**.
   - [ ] **Partograph** is at the end of the chart list on the left.
   - [ ] It is shown only for women. Open a man's screen and check it is not there.
2. Select **Partograph**.
   - [ ] The form has your seven sections:
     1. Patient Information;
     2. Blood Group / Rh Factor;
     3. Labour Progression;
     4. Vital Signs;
     5. Delivery Details;
     6. Baby Information;
     7. Additional Notes.
   - [ ] It starts with **Observation Time**, set to now.
   - [ ] Under the Partograph's title it says *Not started*.

### D2. Labour observations

1. Fill in these fields and press **Save**:
   - **Gravida** 2 and **Para** 1;
   - **Blood Group** O, **Rhesus** Positive, **Hb** 9.5;
   - **Cervical Dilation** 5 and **Fetal Heart Rate** 170;
   - **Contractions per 10 min** 3, **Intensity** Moderate, **Amniotic Fluid** Clear;
   - **BP** 120 / 80 and **Pulse Rate** 90.
   - [ ] *Saved* shows. Under **Partograph** in the chart list it now says *In labour*.
   - [ ] **Hb Flag** reads *Anaemia*. It is worked out from the Hb: under 7 is severe anaemia, under 11 is anaemia.
   - [ ] The **Labour observations** table under the form has one row. Its **Alert** column shows **FHR 170** in red.
   - [ ] Gravida, Para, blood group and Hb are still filled in. Labour Progression and Vital Signs are empty, ready for the next reading.
   - [ ] Boxes you left empty show empty in the table, not 0.
2. Change **Observation Time** to an hour later, enter **Cervical Dilation** 7, and save.
   - [ ] The table has two rows, the newest first.
3. Press **Labour Care Guide**.
   - [ ] The MOH Labour Care Guide opens with both readings plotted.

### D3. Delivery and baby

1. Press **Complete Delivery** before filling in the delivery.
   - [ ] It refuses, and names what is missing: the delivery date and time, the mode of delivery and at least one baby.
2. Fill in these sections and save:
   - **Delivery Details:** Mode of Delivery, AMTSL, Uterotonic, Placenta Complete, Episiotomy / Tear, Repair, Blood Loss, and the mother's pulse, temperature and respiration rate;
   - **Baby Information:** Birth Outcome *Live birth*, Sex, the APGAR scores, Birth Weight, Length and Head Circumference, TEO, Vitamin K;
   - **Additional Notes:** Date & Time of Delivery, Birth Notification Number, Delivered By.
   - [ ] The values are still there after saving, and can be corrected and saved again.
3. Press **Open Delivery Record**.
   - [ ] The delivery record shows the same details: the stages of labour, AMTSL, Repair, the mother after delivery, and the baby's length and head circumference.
4. Go back to the woman's screen and press **Complete Delivery**, then **Yes**.
   - [ ] *Delivery completed* shows, and the chart list says *Delivered*.
   - [ ] The form is gone; the table and the buttons stay.
   - [ ] A patient record *Baby of …* exists for the baby, linked to the mother.

### D4. How your fields are kept (please confirm)

- [ ] **Tetracycline Given** (Delivery Details) and **TEO Given at Birth** (Baby) are the same drug, so they are one field: **TEO (tetracycline) Given at Birth**, in Baby Information.
- [ ] **Frequency** is kept as **Contraction Duration (seconds)**, next to Contractions per 10 min and Intensity.
- [ ] **Fetal Heart Rate** was added to Labour Progression. Your form did not have it, and the partograph's alerts need it.
- [ ] **Uteronic Field** (Baby Information) is not on the form yet. Tell us what it should record.
- [ ] Twins: the form records the first baby. Add a second baby on the delivery record, under **Babies**.

## E. Child welfare: immunisations and the fridge (Nurse)

### E1. Immunisations

1. Open a child's **Patient** record (under 5 years) and press **Immunisations**.
   - [ ] The Kenya schedule is listed for the child's age. Each dose shows **Given**, **Due**, **Overdue**, **Not yet due** or **Too late** (for example OPV 0 after two weeks), with the due date.
   - [ ] A girl of 10 years sees **HPV 1**; a woman sees the **Td** doses.
2. Press **Record Dose** on BCG, enter a batch number, keep **Given at this facility** ticked, and press **Record**.
   - [ ] BCG now shows **Given**, with the date and batch.
   - [ ] Recording BCG again is refused.
3. Record a dose the child had elsewhere: untick **Given at this facility**.
   - [ ] It shows *(from card)*. It completes the schedule but is not counted in MOH 710.
4. As the administrator, open **Setup › Immunisation Dose**.
   - [ ] The schedule is there to edit. Disable *Yellow Fever* or the *Malaria vaccine* doses if your county does not give them.

### E2. The vaccine fridge

1. As the administrator, open **Setup › Cold Chain Fridge** and add your fridge (for example *Electrolux, CWC room*). The safe range is 2 to 8 °C.
2. As a nurse, open **Maternity › Fridge Temperature Reading** and record this morning's temperature, 5 °C.
   - [ ] It saves. A second **Morning** reading for the same fridge and day is refused.
3. Record the evening at **9.5 °C** with no action.
   - [ ] It is refused: write the action taken. With an action, it saves and is marked **Too warm**.
4. Open **Reports › Fridge Temperature Sheet** for the fridge and this month.
   - [ ] One row per day, with the morning and evening temperatures, the time and the initials of who recorded them.
   - [ ] The 9.5 °C is red. The summary at the top counts too warm, too cold and readings not taken.

## F. MOH reports (Records officer or administrator)

### F1. MOH 710 Immunization Summary

1. Open **Reports › MOH 710 Immunization Summary** for this month.
   - [ ] Each dose of the schedule has a row: **Under 1 Year**, **1 Year and Over**, **Total**.
   - [ ] The BCG given in E1 is counted; the dose from the card is not.
   - [ ] The last row is **Fully Immunised Child (under 1 year)**.

### F2. Commodity reports (F-CDRR)

1. Open **Setup › Commodity Report Form**.
   - [ ] **MOH 747A** (family planning), **MOH 730A** and **MOH 730B** (ARV and OI medicines), **MOH 643B** (HIV testing and ART monitoring) and **TB and Leprosy** are there.
   - [ ] **Please compare each one with your current paper form.** Add, rename or remove lines so they match; the forms are yours to edit.
2. In **MOH 747A**, map one line to a stock item: choose **Our Item**, and set **Stock Units per Unit of Issue** (for example 1 cycle = 1, or a pack of 30 tablets = 30). Save.
3. Open **Reports › Commodity Report (F-CDRR)**, choose **MOH 747A** and last month.
   - [ ] Lines are grouped under their sections. Lines not mapped show *Not mapped*.
   - [ ] The mapped line shows Beginning Balance, Received, Dispensed / Used, Losses, Adjustments, Ending Balance, Expiring in 6 Months, Days Out of Stock and Quantity Requested.
   - [ ] Beginning + Received − Dispensed − Losses + Adjustments (+) − Adjustments (−) = Ending Balance.
4. Stock thrown away (expired or damaged): make a **Stock Entry** of type **Expired or Damaged**.
   - [ ] It is counted under **Losses**, not Dispensed.
5. **Store (empty = all):** choose the pharmacy store to report one store only.

**For the commodity reports to show real numbers**, the programme commodities (family planning, ARVs, HIV test kits, TB drugs) must be received and issued in the system's stock. Tell us whether you keep them on separate bin cards today.

**MOH 729B and the HTS tally** need HIV clinic records. If your comprehensive care clinic uses KenyaEMR, those reports come from there; tell us, and we will not duplicate them.

## G. Imaging (Radiographer, Doctor)

1. As the administrator, open **Kenya Health Settings › Imaging (PACS)**. Put your image viewer's link in **Image Viewer Link**, with `{accession}` where the accession number goes. Examples:
   - Orthanc Explorer 2: `http://<orthanc-address>:8042/ui/app/#/filtered-studies?AccessionNumber={accession}`
   - OHIF: `http://<viewer-address>/?accession={accession}`
   - your PACS's own viewer link, if it takes an accession number.
2. On an imaging exam that is **Performed**:
   - [ ] **View Images** is on the exam, and opens the viewer on that exam's study.
   - [ ] The **Radiology Worklist** has an **Images** column with **View**.
   - [ ] On the ward, the patient's **Results** tab has **View Images** next to the exam.
3. Before an exam is performed, there is no View Images.
4. A link typed on one exam (**PACS Viewer Link**) is used for that exam instead.

The viewer opens from staff computers on the hospital network, so your image server needs no opening to the internet. For the pictures to be found, the radiographer types the exam's **Accession** number (shown on the Radiology Worklist) on the X-ray or ultrasound machine. Sending it to the machine automatically (a DICOM worklist) is the next step. For that, tell us the Carestream and Mindray models, and whether they send DICOM.
