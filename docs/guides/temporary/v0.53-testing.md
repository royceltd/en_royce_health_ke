# The Inpatient Screen: a Form and a Table for Every Entry — Testing Guide

This is a short testing script for version 0.53.0. It covers the inpatient part of the feedback of 20 November:
- vital signs on a simple form instead of the large dialog;
- diagnosis and requests entered on the admission;
- doctor's and nursing notes with the same design, kept apart;
- the nursing charts listed on the left, each with its form and a table of entries, with the Fluid Balance, Turning, Blood Transfusion, Fetal Kick and Diabetic/RBS charts to your designs.

The Partograph and the theatre tabs come in version 0.54.

Test on the demo, **hospital.royceltd.com**, with an admitted patient: **Ward** › the patient. Use a Nurse login and a Doctor login as each part says. Tick each item as you go, and send a screenshot if anything is different.

## A. Vitals (Nurse)

1. Open the **Vitals** tab.
   - [ ] A **Record Vital Signs** form is on the tab: Date and Time, Temperature, Pulse, BP systolic and diastolic, Respiratory Rate, SpO2, Weight, Height, Comments.
2. Fill a few readings, a comment, and **Save**.
   - [ ] *Saved*. The reading is at the top of the table: Date Recorded, Weight, Height, BMI, BP, Pulse, Resp. Rate, Temp, SpO2, Comments, Flags.
   - [ ] An out-of-range reading shows in red under Flags.
3. **Save** with only a comment.
   - [ ] *Enter at least one reading.*

## B. Diagnosis (Doctor)

1. Open the **Diagnosis** tab.
   - [ ] An **Add Diagnosis** form is on the tab, not a link to the consultation.
2. Type *malaria* in **Diagnosis** and choose one.
   - [ ] The **ICD-11 Code** fills in. Typing a code fills the name the same way.
3. **Add Diagnosis**.
   - [ ] The diagnosis is in the table with its code, the date and your name.

## C. Requests (Doctor)

1. Open the **Requests** tab.
   - [ ] A **New Request** form is on the tab: Request Type, Service, Urgency, Notes.
2. Choose *Laboratory*, then a test, *Urgent*, a note, then **Send Request**.
   - [ ] The request is in the table, going to the Laboratory, and it is on the Lab Worklist.
3. Do the same for an imaging exam.
   - [ ] It goes to Radiology.

## D. Notes (Doctor, then Nurse)

1. **Doctor Notes** tab, as the doctor.
   - [ ] **Current** and **Past** are on the left. Current has the note box with formatting, **Date**, **Patient Status**, **Notes By** with your name, and the **Quick Note Templates**.
2. Click **Patient Assessment**, write a line under it, choose *Improving*, and **Save**.
   - [ ] Under **Past**, the note shows with the time, your name and the status.
3. **Doctor Instructions** tab.
   - [ ] It has the same design. Its notes are kept apart from the Doctor Notes.
4. **Nursing Notes** tab, as the nurse.
   - [ ] It has the same design, with **Nursing Care**, **Shift Handover** and **Nursing Tasks** on the left too. A nurse cannot write on the Doctor Notes tab: it opens on Past.

## E. Nursing charts (Nurse)

1. Open **Nursing Care**.
   - [ ] The charts are listed on the left, with when each is next due. Choosing one shows its form, then its entries in a table.
2. **Fluid Balance Chart**: fill **Infused** *500*, **Urine** *300*, and **Save**.
   - [ ] The entry is in the table. *Last 24 hours* shows the totals.
3. **Turning Chart**: choose **Position before** *Supine*, **Position turned to** *Left Lateral*, **Save**.
   - [ ] *Next due* moves 2 hours on.
4. **Blood Transfusion**.
   - [ ] The form has Product, Unit number, Stage, Volume, Temperature, Pulse, BP, Reaction, then Diagnosis, Blood group, Rhesus, Expiry date, Started by, Rate, Respiratory rate, Reaction details, Intervention drugs, and **Counter Checked By**.
5. **Fetal Kick Chart** and **Diabetic Chart**.
   - [ ] Fetal Kick has Start and End time, Kicks per hour, Maternal condition and Next review.
   - [ ] Diabetic has FBS, RBS (mg/dl), Urinalysis, Drug type, Dose and Blood sugar frequency.
