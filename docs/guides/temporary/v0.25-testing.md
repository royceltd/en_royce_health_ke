# Uncovered Insured Items and Doctor Assignment — Testing Guide

A short script to test versions 0.24 and 0.25. Version 0.24 makes insured patients pay first for what their scheme doesn't cover. Version 0.25 assigns the next doctor and room automatically. Each test says what to do and what the system should do. Tick each one off. If anything behaves differently, send a screenshot.

Test on the demo hospital (hospital.royceltd.com), not on your live site, because the tests create patients, visits and invoices. Both features are already set up on the demo. To set them up on Bethesda, see [Bethesda setup for versions 0.24 and 0.25](bethesda-setup-v0.25.md).

## Before you start

You need five logins (Royce can create them for you on the demo):

| Login | Roles | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reception | Receptionist | Check-in |
| Nurse | Nursing User | Triage and Send To |
| Doctor | Physician, linked to Dr Brian Kiprotich (his **User** field) | Consultations, My Patients |
| Lab | Laboratory User | Lab tests and imaging |
| Cashier | Cashier | Bills and payments |

Demo records used below:

- **Insurer and scheme:** *Mlima Health Assurance (Demo)*, scheme *Corporate Gold (Demo)*. The scheme covers the consultation, the lab tests and the drugs at 90%. It does **not** cover imaging.
- **Doctors on duty:** Dr Amina Achieng (OPD Room 1) and Dr Brian Kiprotich (OPD Room 2), Monday to Saturday, 08:00 to 17:00. Run section B in those hours.
- **Facility:** Kijani Valley Hospital - Main.

Write down the patient, visit (VIS-…) and invoice (ACC-SINV-…) numbers you create.

## A. Insured patients pay first for what isn't covered

Guide: [Reception, queue and billing](../12-reception-queue-billing.md), Step 7.

As **Reception**, register a new adult patient and check them in: *Outpatient*, General Consultation, **Practitioner left blank**, payment **Insurance**, payer *Mlima Health Assurance (Demo)*, scheme *Corporate Gold (Demo)*, a new member number, cover valid until next year.

| # | Do this | The system should |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | As **Doctor**, **Record › Consultation** on the visit. Order *White Cell Count (WBC)* and the imaging exam *Chest X-ray (PA)*, and prescribe *Tablet Paracetamol 500mg*. Submit. | A **Patient pays first** message: *Not covered by Corporate Gold (Demo)*, listing the Chest X-ray only. |
| 2 | Open the visit's **Bill** section. | The X-ray reads *Not covered: patient pays first*. The consultation, WBC and paracetamol read *Insurer's bill*, with 90% in the Insurance column. The totals show **Patient pays now**: the X-ray's price. |
| 3 | Open the visit's draft invoice from the Bill section (*Bill For: Insurer*). | It has the consultation, WBC and paracetamol, not the X-ray. |
| 4 | As **Lab**, start the WBC test from the doctor's order. | It starts: the scheme covers it, so the patient is served first. |
| 5 | As **Lab**, try to perform the X-ray. | Refused: *… is not covered by the patient's insurance, so it is paid first. Send the patient to the Cashier …* |
| 6 | As **Nurse**, press **Send To** on the visit. | The station offered first is **Cashier**. |
| 7 | As **Cashier**, **Bill › Create Bill** on the visit. | A new invoice with only the X-ray, marked *Bill For: Patient*, and no insurance share. |
| 8 | Submit it without taking payment. As **Lab**, try the X-ray again. | Still refused: billed is not paid. |
| 9 | As **Cashier**, take the full payment (**Create › Payment**). As **Lab**, perform the X-ray. | It goes ahead. |
| 10 | As **Cashier**, **Bill › Create Bill** again. | Refused: everything left is covered by insurance and is on the insurer's bill. |
| 11 | **Status › Complete Visit**. | The insurer's bill is submitted, with the copay left for the cashier. The X-ray is not on it. |

## B. The next doctor and room

Guide: [Reception, queue and billing](../12-reception-queue-billing.md), Step 3.

Check in three new cash patients: *Outpatient*, General Consultation, **Practitioner left blank**.

| # | Do this | The system should |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 12 | As **Nurse**, on the first patient, **Send To** Consultation with *Practitioner* blank. | A message names the doctor and room, for example *Sent to Amina Achieng, OPD Room 1 - KVHL*. The top of the visit shows *Consultation: doctor · room · General Consultation*. The **Room** field is filled. |
| 13 | Do the same for the second patient. | The other doctor is picked, in the other room: the patients are shared out. |
| 14 | Do the same for the third patient. | The doctor with fewer patients waiting is picked (back to the first doctor if both have one). |
| 15 | On the third patient, **View › Queue Slip**. | A slip with the room, doctor, service and **Patients ahead of you: 1** (the first patient is waiting for the same doctor). |
| 16 | On the second patient, **Status › Reassign Doctor**, confirm. | The patient goes to the other doctor. Under Movements the last line notes *Reassigned from …*. The waiting time is unchanged. |
| 17 | Check in a fourth patient, and **Send To** Consultation choosing **Dr Brian Kiprotich** by hand. | Brian is kept, with his room, whatever the queue. |
| 18 | Log in as **Doctor** (Brian). Kenya Health › Patient Visit › **My Patients**. | Only the open visits sent to Brian at Consultation. |
| 19 | Outside duty hours (after 17:00, or on Sunday), send a new patient to Consultation with *Practitioner* blank. | Refused: *No doctor … is on duty now … Choose the doctor.* Choosing a doctor by hand works. |

## C. Switched off

As **Manager**, Kenya Health › Kenya Health Settings › Payment Before Service. Untick **Insured: uncovered items paid first** and save. Tick it again when this section is done.

| # | Do this | The system should |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 20 | Repeat tests 1 and 5 with a new insured patient. | No *Patient pays first* message. The X-ray can be performed unpaid: the whole insured visit is served first, as before version 0.24. |
