# Data Protection: Consent, Patients' Requests and the Breach Register — Testing Guide

This is a short testing script for version 0.46.0. It covers what the Data Protection Act asks of a hospital's system:

- the patient is told how their information is used, and asked about SMS, at registration, with a history of every consent;
- a patient's request for a copy of their record, a correction or deletion is logged and answered on time, and the copy is produced by the system;
- personal data breaches are recorded, and the Data Commissioner (ODPC) is told within 72 hours.

Test on the demo, **hospital.royceltd.com**. Sign in as the Administrator for part A, then with the role logins. Tick each item as you go, and send a screenshot if anything is different.

## A. Setup (Administrator)

1. Kenya Health Settings › **Data Protection**.
   - [ ] **Privacy notice and SMS question at registration** is ticked.
   - [ ] **Days to Answer a Request for a Copy** is 7, and **Days to Answer Other Requests** is 14. Your Data Protection Officer confirms these; change them if they advise otherwise.
2. Choose a user in **Data Protection Officer**, and save.
3. Give that user the role **Data Protection Officer** (User › Roles).
4. Kenya Health › Setup › **Compliance Check**.
   - [ ] Four new **Data protection** rows show: registration consent (Pass), a named Data Protection Officer (Pass once set), requests answered on time (Pass), breaches notified within 72 hours (Pass).

## B. Consent at registration (Reception)

1. Patient › **+ Add**. Fill in the usual details but leave **Privacy Notice Given** unticked, and save.
   - [ ] Refused: tell the patient how their information is used, then tick Privacy Notice Given.
2. Read the text under **Privacy Notice Given** to the "patient", tick it, and set **Agrees to Receive SMS** as they answer. Save.
   - [ ] The patient saves. **Consent Recorded At** and **Consent Recorded By** (you) fill in by themselves.
3. Register a child: fill in **Consent Given By** with the parent's name and **Relation to the Patient** *Parent*.
   - [ ] Saved with the parent's name.
4. Open the first patient again, untick **Agrees to Receive SMS**, save.
   - [ ] **Consent Recorded At** changes to now.
5. Kenya Health › Setup › **Patient Consent Log**.
   - [ ] The first patient has three entries: privacy notice *Given*, SMS *Given*, then SMS *Withdrawn*, each with who and when.
   - [ ] Open one and try to change it: refused. Entries are never changed or deleted.
6. Send a results-ready SMS or an SMS campaign to that patient.
   - [ ] They no longer get it.

## C. A patient asks for a copy of their record (Reception, then Data Protection Officer)

1. **Reception:** Kenya Health › Setup › **Patient Data Requests** › **+ Add**:
   - **Patient**: one with visits and lab results;
   - **Request**: *Access (copy of the record)*;
   - **What Is Asked**: *A copy of all my records*;
   - under **Who Asked**, tick **Identity Checked** and say how (*ID seen*).
   Save.
   - [ ] **Reply Due** is 7 days from today, and **Received By** is you.
2. **Data Protection Officer:** open the request and press **Export Patient Record**.
   - [ ] Two files are attached to the request: a **PDF** (the patient file: history, every visit, vital signs, diagnoses, medicines, results) and a **JSON** file (the same in a format another system can read).
   - [ ] The status is now **In Progress**.
   - [ ] **Reception** does not see the Export button.
3. Set **Status** to *Completed* with **What Was Done** empty, and save.
   - [ ] Refused: say what was done.
4. Fill in **What Was Done** (*Printed copy handed to the patient*) and save as *Completed*.
   - [ ] **Completed On** and **Completed By** fill in.
5. **Administrator:** Kenya Health › Setup › **Patient Record Access**.
   - [ ] The export is logged as **Export**, with who and when.

## D. A patient asks for a correction (Reception, Data Protection Officer)

1. New request: **Request** *Correction*, **What Is Asked** *My date of birth is wrong: it is 12 March 1985*.
   - [ ] **Reply Due** is 14 days from today.
2. **Data Protection Officer:** press **Open Patient to Correct**, fix the date of birth on the patient, save.
3. Back on the request, write in **What Was Done** the field, the old and the new value, and complete it.
   - [ ] On the patient, the activity timeline at the bottom of the form shows the date of birth changed from the old to the new value, by whom and when.
4. Make a request with **Status** *Refused* and no reason.
   - [ ] Refused until **Reason for Refusing** is filled in.

## E. Overdue requests

1. New request: **Received On** set to 30 days ago, status *Open*.
   - [ ] **Compliance Check**: the row *Patients' requests answered on time* fails and names the request.
2. The next morning, the Data Protection Officer and the Healthcare Administrators get a notification: *1 patient data request(s) past their reply date*.

## F. The breach register (any staff, then the Data Protection Officer)

1. **Any staff member** (for example the Cashier): type *New Data Breach* in the search bar. The Data Protection Officer finds the register under Kenya Health › Setup › **Data Breach Register**. Fill in:
   - **What Happened**: *Sent to the wrong person*;
   - **Description**: *A lab result was emailed to the wrong patient*.
   Save.
   - [ ] **ODPC Notice Due** is 72 hours after **Discovered At**.
   - [ ] The Cashier sees only the breaches they reported.
2. **Data Protection Officer:** fill in **Risk to Patients**, **Data Affected**, **Patients Affected** and **Steps Taken to Contain It**, then set **Status** to *Closed*.
   - [ ] Refused: record when the Data Commissioner was notified.
3. Fill in **ODPC Notified At** and **ODPC Reference**. Try to close again.
   - [ ] Refused until **What Changes So It Doesn't Happen Again** is filled in. Then it closes, and **Closed At** fills in.
4. Make a breach with **Discovered At** four days ago and no notice.
   - [ ] **Compliance Check**: *Data breaches notified to the Data Commissioner within 72 hours* fails and names it.
   - [ ] The daily notification warns the Data Protection Officer about breaches whose 72 hours end within a day.
5. Untick **ODPC Must Be Notified** on a breach and save without a reason.
   - [ ] Refused until **Why Not Notified** is filled in (for example *encrypted laptop, key kept separately*).
