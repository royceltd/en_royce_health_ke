# Version 0.58: Your Feedback of 21 November — Testing Guide

This testing script covers version 0.58.0, the fixes from your feedback of 21 November:

- **Consultation requests:** the Requests tab now has the same form and table as the ward. You choose the request type, the service (with the patient's price), the urgency and a note, then press **Add Request**.
- **Diagnosis:**
  - the consultation's **ICD-11 Code** column shows the code (for example *1F03*), not the name again;
  - every diagnosis on the ward needs an ICD-11 code.
- **Ward drugs:**
  - the prescription form is always shown. When you can't prescribe, it says why;
  - a new **Give Dose** form on the **Administration Log**.
- **Partograph:**
  - the first entry saves even when the pregnancy details are left empty. This caused the error you saw;
  - the form no longer fills in answers nobody gave: mode of delivery, ticks shown as *No*, Hb *0.0*.

Test on the demo, **hospital.royceltd.com**. Tick each item as you go, and send a screenshot if anything is different.

## Before you start

- **Reload each page fully after an update:** press **Ctrl+Shift+R** (on a Mac, **Cmd+Shift+R**). Without it, your browser may keep the old screen, and forms can look stuck or empty.
- **For part A:** an outpatient consultation that is open, with the Observation tab filled in.
- **For parts B to D:** an admitted patient on the **General Ward**.
- **For part E:** a woman admitted to the **Maternity Ward**. Your test patient, Tess Mbaabu, will do.

## A. Requests on the consultation (Doctor)

1. Open the consultation and go to the **Requests** tab.
   - [ ] At the top is a **New Request** form: **Request Type**, **Service**, **Urgency** and **Notes**. Under it is a table of this consultation's requests.
   - [ ] The old Lab Tests, Procedures and Therapies tables are no longer shown.
2. Choose Request Type **Imaging**, then open the **Service** list.
   - [ ] Only imaging exams are offered, each with this patient's price.
3. Choose an exam, set Urgency **Urgent**, write a note and press **Add Request**.
   - [ ] The consultation saves, and the exam is in the table with its type, urgency and note.
   - [ ] Its status is **Not sent** (orange), with a **Remove** button. If your hospital sends requests on save, it shows the request's status instead.
4. Add a lab test the same way (Request Type **Lab Test**).
5. Press **Remove** on one request that is not sent.
   - [ ] It leaves the table.
6. Press **Send Requests**.
   - [ ] Each request shows its status and **Goes to** (Laboratory, Radiology and so on). **Remove** is gone.
7. Under the table, press **Show the full tables**.
   - [ ] The old tables come back, for anything the form doesn't cover. **Hide the full tables** hides them again.

## B. Diagnosis (Doctor)

### B1. On the consultation

1. On the **Diagnosis** tab, add a row and choose a diagnosis with an ICD-11 code, for example *Measles*.
   - [ ] The **ICD-11 Code** column shows the code, *1F03*, not *Measles*.
2. Save and reload the page (Ctrl+Shift+R).
   - [ ] The column still shows the code.

### B2. On the ward

1. On the admitted patient's screen, open **Diagnosis**.
2. Choose a diagnosis that has no ICD-11 code yet, leave **ICD-11 Code** empty, and press **Add Diagnosis**.
   - [ ] You are told the diagnosis has no ICD-11 code yet, and nothing is added.
3. Choose its ICD-11 code, then press **Add Diagnosis**.
   - [ ] It is added with the code, and the diagnosis keeps its own name.
   - [ ] Next time you choose that diagnosis, the code fills in by itself.
4. Choose a diagnosis that already has a code, for example *Measles*.
   - [ ] The code fills in by itself, and the diagnosis is added.

The demo's older diagnoses were given their code where the name matches an ICD-11 title exactly.

## C. The ward prescription form (Nurse, then Doctor)

1. Log in as a nurse. On the patient's screen, open **Drug Administration** › **Prescription**.
   - [ ] The **Add Prescription** form shows on the left, greyed out.
   - [ ] A note above it says *Only doctors prescribe*.
2. Log in as a user who is not linked to a Healthcare Practitioner (for example the administrator).
   - [ ] The note says your user is not linked to a Healthcare Practitioner and how to link it.
   - [ ] To link it, the administrator opens **Healthcare Practitioner**, opens the doctor's record, sets the **User** field to the doctor's login and saves.
3. Log in as a doctor linked to a Healthcare Practitioner.
   - [ ] The form works as before: **Add Prescription**, then **Save Prescription**.

## D. Give Dose on the Administration Log (Nurse)

You need a drug the pharmacy has sent to the bed (Drug Administration › Dispensing shows it as **at the bedside**).

1. Open **Drug Administration** › **Administration Log**.
   - [ ] A **Give Dose** form sits above the treatment sheet. **Medication Round** is still there as a button.
2. Open the **Dose** list.
   - [ ] The doses due are listed with their time, drug and dose. A held or refused dose shows its status in brackets.
   - [ ] If no dose is due, the form says so: a dose shows once the pharmacy has sent the drug to the bed.
3. Choose a dose. Leave Status **Given**, check the **Route**, and press **Record Dose**.
   - [ ] The dose shows in the treatment sheet as **Given**, with the time and your name.
4. Choose another dose, set Status **Held**, leave the comment empty, and press **Record Dose**.
   - [ ] You are asked to say why the dose was not given.
5. Write the reason and press **Record Dose** again.
   - [ ] The dose shows as **Held** with your comment.

## E. Partograph (Midwife or Nurse)

1. Reload the page with **Ctrl+Shift+R**. On the woman's screen, open **Nursing Charts** › **Partograph**.
   - [ ] The form opens. If anything goes wrong, a red message says what happened and what to do; the panel is never blank.
2. For a woman with no partograph yet, leave the pregnancy details empty. Enter only a cervical dilatation and a fetal heart rate, then press **Save**.
   - [ ] The entry saves and shows in the observations table.
3. Look at the **Delivery Details** and **Baby** sections.
   - [ ] **Mode of delivery** is empty until someone chooses it.
   - [ ] The Yes/No questions (AMTSL, placenta complete, TEO, vitamin K and others) are empty, not *No*.
   - [ ] **Hb** is empty, not *0.0*, until someone enters it.

Draft deliveries that took *Spontaneous vaginal delivery* by themselves, with no delivery time recorded yet, now have the mode of delivery cleared. Tess Mbaabu's is one of them if her delivery time is still empty.
