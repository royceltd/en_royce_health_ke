# Take Payment, Cashier Before Service Points and the Billing Menu — Testing Guide

A short script for version 0.37, the rest of your notes of 17 November:
- **Take Payment**: type the amount received; the system shows the change and pays the bill;
- after the doctor, a **cash** patient goes to the **Cashier** first, then on to the lab, radiology or pharmacy once paid. Insured patients go straight on;
- one **Billing** menu for the cashier.

Each test says what to do and what the system should do. Tick each one off. If anything behaves differently, send a screenshot.

Test on your own system (**bethesda.royceltd.com**) with your staff logins. Use new test patients with "Test" in the name. The cashier needs an open **Cashier Shift** if your hospital requires one.

## A. The Billing menu (cashier)

1. Log in as the **Cashier** and open **Kenya Health** in the sidebar.
   - [ ] A **Billing** section lists **Patient Bills**, **Sales Invoice**, **Payment Entry**, **Cashier Shift** and **Bill Approval Request**.

Who bills: the **Cashier** role. Only cashiers (and accounts managers) take money; the lab, pharmacy and other service points can bill but not receive payment, when Kenya Health Settings › Cashiers › *Only cashiers take payments* is ticked.

## B. Take Payment with change (cash)

1. **Reception:** check in a cash test patient (*Outpatient*). The visit waits at the Cashier for the KES 200 consultation fee.
2. **Cashier:** open the visit › **Bill** › **Create Bill** › **Create Invoice**. **Save** the invoice.
3. Press **Take Payment**. *Paid By* = Cash, **Amount Received** = 500.
   - [ ] The box shows **Change to give: KES 300**.
4. Press **Take Payment**.
   - [ ] "Payment taken": Paid 200, **Change to give 300**. The invoice is submitted and **Paid**.
   - [ ] **Print Receipt**: the receipt shows *Amount Tendered 500* and *Change 300*.
   - [ ] The visit has moved to **Triage**.

## C. Take Payment by M-Pesa, and part payment

1. On another bill, **Take Payment** with *Paid By* = M-Pesa and no transaction code.
   - [ ] Refused: enter the transaction code.
2. Type a code and an amount **more** than the bill.
   - [ ] Refused: change is given only for cash.
3. Type the code and the exact amount.
   - [ ] Paid. The receipt shows the M-Pesa code.
4. On another bill, take **less** than the total in cash.
   - [ ] "Still owed" shows the balance; the bill stays *Partly Paid*, and **Take Payment** is offered again for the rest.

## D. Cash patient: doctor → Cashier → lab

1. Take the cash patient of part B to the doctor. The **doctor** requests a lab test, then **Complete Consultation** (or **Send Requests**).
   - [ ] "Where does the patient go next?" suggests **Cashier**, saying the requests are paid first.
2. Choose **Laboratory** instead and **Send**.
   - [ ] The message says the patient was sent to the **Cashier first**. The visit is at the Cashier, and **After Paying, Goes To** = *Laboratory*.
   - [ ] **Lab:** the test is not on the Lab Worklist yet.
3. **Cashier:** on the visit, **Send To** › *Laboratory*.
   - [ ] Refused: the requests are not paid yet.
4. **Bill** › **Create Bill** › **Create Invoice**, save, **Take Payment**.
   - [ ] As soon as it is paid, the visit moves to **Laboratory, waiting** ("Paid at the Cashier").
   - [ ] **Lab:** the test is on the Lab Worklist.
5. Repeat with a **prescription only** (no lab test).
   - [ ] The doctor's "next" suggests the **Cashier**; once paid, the patient moves to the **Pharmacy**.

## E. Insured patient goes straight on

1. Check in an **insured** test patient (for example Mwalimu). The doctor requests a lab test and sends the patient to **Laboratory**.
   - [ ] The patient goes **straight to the Laboratory**, not the Cashier, and the test is on the Lab Worklist.
