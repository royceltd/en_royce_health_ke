# Paying Before Service: Consultation Fee, Billing Desk and Service Points — Testing Guide

A script to test version 0.35, the payment part of your outpatient flow feedback of 16 November:
- a cash patient pays the consultation fee at the Cashier before triage sees them;
- the cashier sees what the doctor requested, ticks what the patient pays for now, and invoices it;
- the lab and radiology see a cash patient's request only once it is paid.

Insured patients are not stopped: they go straight to triage and to every service point, as before.

Each test says what to do and what the system should do. Tick each one off. If anything behaves differently, send a screenshot.

Test on your own system (**bethesda.royceltd.com**) with your staff logins. The same changes are on the demo hospital (hospital.royceltd.com).

## Before you start

| Login | Used for |
| --- | --- |
| Reception | Check-in |
| Cashier | Billing and payment |
| Nurse | Triage |
| Doctor | Consultation and requests |
| Lab | Lab Worklist |
| Radiology | Radiology Worklist |
| Manager | Allowing services before payment |

Check, once, as the manager:
- [ ] Kenya Health Settings › **Payment Before Service**: **Consultation fee before triage**, **Laboratory** and **Radiology** are ticked.
- [ ] Every consulting doctor has a consultation charge (Healthcare › Practitioner › Charges), and the Service Type (for example *General Consultation*) has a charge for the department. Without a charge there is no fee to pay, and the patient goes straight to triage.

Use new test patients with "Test" in the name.

## A. Consultation fee before triage

1. **Reception:** check in a test patient: Visit Type *Outpatient*, Service Type *General Consultation*, Payment *Cash*. Choose the doctor, or leave it blank. **Save**.
   - [ ] The visit is **waiting at the Cashier**, not at Triage. The movement says "Consultation fee before triage".
   - [ ] The top of the visit says the consultation fee is to be paid at the Cashier.
   - [ ] **Nurse:** the patient is **not** on the Triage waiting list (Patient Visit, Station = Triage).
2. **Reception or nurse:** try **Send To** › *Triage* on that visit.
   - [ ] Refused: the consultation fee is not paid yet.
3. **Cashier:** open the visit (Patient Visit, Station = Cashier). Under **Bill**, the consultation fee is listed, at the doctor's charge (or the service's charge when no doctor was chosen).
4. **Bill** › **Create Bill**: the fee is ticked. **Create Invoice**, take the payment on the invoice and submit it. Print the receipt.
   - [ ] As soon as the invoice is paid, the visit moves to **Triage, waiting**: the movement says "Consultation fee paid".
   - [ ] **Nurse:** the patient is now on the Triage list.
5. Triage sends the patient to **Consultation**, and the doctor consults.
   - [ ] The consultation is **not billed a second time**: the visit's bill shows no new consultation fee.

Who goes straight to triage, without paying first:
- [ ] An **insured** patient (Payment *Insurance*).
- [ ] *Casualty*, *Lab only*, *Radiology only* and *Pharmacy only* visits (they go straight to their service point).
- [ ] A patient a **manager** allowed through: on the visit, **Bill** › **Allow Services Before Payment** with a reason. Then **Send To** › *Triage* works.

## B. Requests: billing desk and service points

1. **Doctor:** for the cash patient of part A, request a lab test (for example *Full Haemogram*) and an X-ray or ultrasound, and submit the consultation. **Send To** the next station.
   - [ ] **Send To** suggests the **Cashier**, as the patient has requests to pay for.
2. **Lab:** open the **Lab Worklist** (From = today).
   - [ ] The patient's test is **not** listed.
   - [ ] Below the list: "1 more patient(s) have tests requested and are paying at the Cashier."
3. **Radiology:** open the **Radiology Worklist**.
   - [ ] The exam is **not** listed, with a note that exams are waiting for payment at the Cashier.
4. **Cashier:** open the visit. **Bill** shows the lab test and the exam under *Not billed yet*.
5. **Bill** › **Create Bill**. Every request is ticked, with the total.
   - [ ] Untick the X-ray: the total goes down.
   - [ ] **Create Invoice** opens an invoice with the lab test only. Take payment, submit, print the receipt.
6. **Lab:** refresh the Lab Worklist.
   - [ ] The test is now listed, ready to collect.
7. **Radiology:** refresh the Radiology Worklist.
   - [ ] The exam is **still not** listed (not paid).
8. **Cashier:** **Create Bill** again: only the X-ray is left. Invoice it and take payment.
   - [ ] **Radiology:** the exam is now listed.

An insured patient's requests appear at the lab and radiology straight away, without going to the Cashier.

## C. Pharmacy (unchanged)

The pharmacist still sees every prescription, paid or not, to check stock and bill the drugs (**Bill Drugs**). The patient pays at the Cashier, then the pharmacist dispenses.
- [ ] **Pharmacist:** a cash patient's prescription is on the Pharmacy Worklist before it is paid.
- [ ] Dispensing is refused until the drugs are paid.
