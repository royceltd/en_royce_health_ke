# Versions 0.50 to 0.53: Your Feedback of 20 November — Testing Guide

This testing script covers everything released from version 0.50 to version 0.53.0, which is now on the demo and on your site:

- **Quick fixes:**
  - screens kept off the sidebar;
  - triage without Tongue, Abdomen and Reflexes;
  - the consultation's Observation tab side by side, in your order;
  - diagnosis by ICD-11 name or code;
  - Counter Checked By on blood transfusions;
  - the WHO Time Out as separate questions;
  - a report of OPD patients by payment mode.
- **The drug screen to your design** (page 3 of your feedback): Add Prescription on the left, Current Prescription on the right, the Medication Mapping note and Save Prescription, on the consultation and on the ward.
- **The inpatient screen, a form and a table for every entry:** vitals, diagnosis, requests, doctor's and nursing notes, and the nursing charts to your designs.

The parts follow the patient: triage, consultation, pharmacy, ward, theatre, reports. Test on the demo, **hospital.royceltd.com**. Use the login each part names. Tick each item as you go, and send a screenshot if anything is different.

Not in these versions yet:
- the **Partograph** and the **theatre list tabs** (version 0.54);
- the full **OPD → Admission → Ward → Theatre / Maternity** hand-over check (version 0.55);
- **Radiology:** tell us what is still pending there.

## Before you start

- **Patients:** you need one patient who has paid the consultation fee (part B) and one admitted patient on a ward (part E).
- **A drug package**, for part C3. As the administrator, open Healthcare Setup › **Treatment Plan Template**, make *Malaria (adult)* and add two drugs to its **Drugs** table, with dose, frequency, duration and route.

## A. Every screen (any login)

1. Open the **Lab Worklist**, the **Pharmacy Worklist**, the **Ward** and an admitted patient's screen.
   - [ ] On each one, the content starts a little way from the sidebar instead of touching it.
   - [ ] A wide table scrolls left and right inside the page, and nothing runs under the sidebar.

## B. Triage (Nurse)

1. Open **Triage Queue** and record vital signs for the patient.
   - [ ] **Tongue**, **Abdomen** and **Reflexes** are not on the form.
   - [ ] Temperature, pulse, respiratory rate, blood pressure, SpO2, height and weight are still there.

## C. The consultation (Doctor)

### C1. Observation

1. Open the patient's consultation, then **Observation**.
   - [ ] There is one form, with no headings and no *"From the patient's record…"* note.
   - [ ] The fields are side by side in this order:
     - **Chief Complaint** | **History of Presenting Illness**
     - **Review of Systems** | **Past Medical History**, **Past Surgical History**
     - **Family History** | **Social History**
     - **OBS/Gynae** (women only)
     - **Physical Examination**, last.
   - [ ] Allergies and Current Medication are not on this tab. They are still on the patient's record.
2. Open a consultation for a man.
   - [ ] **OBS/Gynae** is not shown.

### C2. Diagnosis by name or code

1. On the **Diagnosis** tab, add a row and type *malaria* in **Diagnosis**.
   - [ ] ICD-11 matches are offered. Choosing one fills the **ICD-11 Code**.
2. Add another row and type a code, for example *1F40*, in **ICD-11 Code**.
   - [ ] The **Diagnosis** fills with its name.

The full ICD-11 list, 35,664 codes, is loaded on the demo and on your site.

### C3. The drug screen

1. Open the **Prescription** tab.
   - [ ] **Add Prescription** is on the left. **Current Prescription** is on the right, with a list at the top: *Pending, Sent, Active Prescriptions, Past, Chronic*.
2. Choose a **Drug Class**, then type in **Drug Name**.
   - [ ] Only that class's drugs are offered, each with *Available: N*. Once one is chosen, a green or red **Available** badge shows its stock.
3. Fill in **Dose** *5*, **Dose Unit** *ml*, **Frequency** *TID*, **Duration** *10*, **Route** *Oral*. Keep **Start Date** and **Start Time**.
   - [ ] **Quantity** and **Dispensing Unit** fill in, for example *2* *Bottle* for a 100 ml bottle.
   - [ ] A green **Medication Mapping** note says how it is dispensed, for example *… will be dispensed as 2 Bottle, not 150 ml*.
4. Change **Quantity** to *1*, then **+ Add Prescription**.
   - [ ] The drug is in the table under **Pending**: #, Drug Name, Dose, Frequency, Duration, Route, Quantity *1 Bottle*, with view and delete.
   - [ ] The form clears and keeps the drug class.
5. Tick **Schedule dosage**, put *Every 8 hours*, and add a second drug.
   - [ ] Its frequency shows *every 8 Hour*.
6. Click the eye on a row.
   - [ ] Its details open. Click the bin on the other row.
   - [ ] It is removed.
7. Close the consultation and open it again.
   - [ ] The pending drug is still there: drugs added are kept as a draft. The Pharmacy Worklist does not show it yet.
8. Click **Complete Consultation**.
   - [ ] It is refused: *These drugs are still in draft…*.
9. **Save Prescription**.
   - [ ] *1 drug(s) sent to the pharmacy*. The **Sent** list shows it as *Waiting*.
10. Click **Drug Package**, choose *Malaria (adult)*, then **+ Add Prescription**.
    - [ ] Both drugs of the package are under **Pending**.
11. Add a drug with **Buy Externally** ticked and another with **Chronic medicine** ticked, then **Save Prescription**.
    - [ ] Under **Sent**, the first shows *Buy Outside*: it isn't billed and the pharmacy doesn't dispense it.
12. Complete the consultation. On the patient's next visit, open the Prescription tab.
    - [ ] **Active Prescriptions** lists the drugs still within their course.
    - [ ] **Chronic** lists the chronic one with **Continue**. Clicking it fills the form again.

## D. Pharmacy (Pharmacist)

1. Open the patient from part C on the **Pharmacy Worklist**.
   - [ ] Each drug shows the dose, frequency, duration and route as the doctor wrote them, and the quantity from the drug screen.
   - [ ] The *Buy Externally* drug is under *Buy Outside*, not *Waiting*.

## E. The ward (Doctor and Nurse)

Open **Ward**, then the admitted patient.

### E1. Vitals (Nurse)

1. Open the **Vitals** tab.
   - [ ] A **Record Vital Signs** form is on the tab: Date and Time, Temperature, Pulse, BP systolic and diastolic, Respiratory Rate, SpO2, Weight, Height, Comments.
2. Fill a few readings, a comment, and **Save**.
   - [ ] *Saved*. The reading is at the top of the table: Date Recorded, Weight, Height, BMI, BP, Pulse, Resp. Rate, Temp, SpO2, Comments, Flags.
   - [ ] An out-of-range reading shows in red under Flags.
3. **Save** with only a comment.
   - [ ] *Enter at least one reading.*

### E2. Diagnosis (Doctor)

1. Open the **Diagnosis** tab.
   - [ ] An **Add Diagnosis** form is on the tab, not a link to the consultation.
2. Type *malaria* in **Diagnosis** and choose one.
   - [ ] The **ICD-11 Code** fills in. Typing a code fills the name the same way.
3. **Add Diagnosis**.
   - [ ] The diagnosis is in the table with its code, the date and your name.

### E3. Requests (Doctor)

1. Open the **Requests** tab.
   - [ ] A **New Request** form is on the tab: Request Type, Service, Urgency, Notes.
2. Choose *Laboratory*, then a test, *Urgent*, a note, then **Send Request**.
   - [ ] The request is in the table, going to the Laboratory, and it is on the Lab Worklist.
3. Do the same for an imaging exam.
   - [ ] It goes to Radiology.

### E4. Notes (Doctor, then Nurse)

1. **Doctor Notes** tab, as the doctor.
   - [ ] **Current** and **Past** are on the left. Current has the note box with formatting, **Date**, **Patient Status**, **Notes By** with your name, and the **Quick Note Templates**.
2. Click **Patient Assessment**, write a line under it, choose *Improving*, and **Save**.
   - [ ] Under **Past**, the note shows with the time, your name and the status.
3. **Doctor Instructions** tab.
   - [ ] It has the same design. Its notes are kept apart from the Doctor Notes.
4. **Nursing Notes** tab, as the nurse.
   - [ ] It has the same design, with **Nursing Care**, **Shift Handover** and **Nursing Tasks** on the left too.
   - [ ] A nurse can't write on the Doctor Notes tab: it opens on Past.

### E5. Drug Administration (Doctor, then Nurse)

1. Open **Drug Administration** as the doctor.
   - [ ] On the left: **Prescription**, **Dispensing**, **Administration Log**.
2. **Prescription**: the same screen as the consultation. Add a drug every 8 hours for 2 days, starting at 14:00, then **Save Prescription**.
   - [ ] *1 drug(s) sent to the pharmacy*. The drug is under **Sent today**.
3. **Dispensing**.
   - [ ] The drug shows 5 doses waiting for the pharmacy: today 14:00 and 22:00, tomorrow 06:00, 14:00 and 22:00.
   - [ ] **Stop**, with a reason, stops the doses not given.
4. **Administration Log**, as the nurse.
   - [ ] The treatment sheet lists each dose, given or not, and by whom. **Medication Round** opens the dose recording.
   - [ ] On **Prescription**, the nurse sees the lists but not the **Add Prescription** form.

### E6. Nursing charts (Nurse)

1. Open **Nursing Care**.
   - [ ] The charts are listed on the left, with when each is next due. Choosing one shows its form, then its entries in a table.
2. **Fluid Balance Chart**: fill **Infused** *500*, **Urine** *300*, and **Save**.
   - [ ] The entry is in the table. *Last 24 hours* shows the totals.
3. **Turning Chart**: choose **Position before** *Supine*, **Position turned to** *Left Lateral*, **Save**.
   - [ ] *Next due* moves 2 hours on.
4. **Blood Transfusion**.
   - [ ] The heading says *Counter-checked by a second nurse* in grey. There is no orange label.
   - [ ] The form has Product, Unit number, Stage, Volume, Temperature, Pulse, BP, Reaction, then Diagnosis, Blood group, Rhesus, Expiry date, Started by, Rate, Respiratory rate, Reaction details, Intervention drugs, and **Counter Checked By**.
5. Fill the readings, leave **Counter Checked By** empty, and **Save**.
   - [ ] The form asks for **Counter Checked By**.
6. Choose yourself and save.
   - [ ] *Counter Checked By must be another nurse, not you.*
7. Choose another nurse and save.
   - [ ] The entry is saved.
8. **Fetal Kick Chart** and **Diabetic Chart**.
   - [ ] Fetal Kick has Start and End time, Kicks per hour, Maternal condition and Next review.
   - [ ] Diabetic has FBS, RBS (mg/dl), Urinalysis, Drug type, Dose and Blood sugar frequency.

## F. Theatre: WHO Time Out (Theatre nurse)

1. Open a theatre booking's **Surgical Safety Checklist**, complete **Sign In**, then go to **Time Out**.
   - [ ] **Surgeon:** *Procedure Steps* (Critical, Non-routine or Routine), *Expected Duration* and *Anticipated Blood Loss* are separate fields.
   - [ ] **Anaesthetist:** *Patient-Specific Concerns*, Yes or No. A **Yes** opens a box for the concerns.
   - [ ] **Nursing:** *Sterility Confirmed*, Yes or No, and *Equipment Issues or Concerns*, Yes or No. A **Yes** opens a box for the details.
2. Answer **Yes** to equipment issues, leave the box empty, enter who led the Time Out and save.
   - [ ] It asks for the details.
3. Write the details and save.
   - [ ] Time Out shows *Done*, with the time.
4. **Print › Theatre Checklist**.
   - [ ] Each Time Out answer is printed on its own line.

## G. Reports: OPD patients by payment mode (Reception, Cashier or Administrator)

1. Kenya Health › Reports › **OPD Patients by Payment Mode**.
   - [ ] Today's visits are counted per payment mode: *Cash* first, then each scheme (for example *SHA - PHC*, *SHA - MWALIMU*) or insurer, with a total row and a bar chart.
   - [ ] **Patients** counts each patient once, even with two visits.
2. Change **From Date** and **To Date** to a week, and **Visit Type** to *Outpatient*.
   - [ ] The counts change to match.
