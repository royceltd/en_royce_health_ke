# Pharmacy Worklist — Testing Guide

A short script to test version 0.28: the pharmacy's worklist (prescriptions and counter sales waiting to be dispensed), billing drugs for the Cashier, dispensing in full or in part with medicine labels, buying outside, sending a prescription back to the doctor, returns, and walk-in counter sales. Each test says what to do and what the system should do. Tick each one off. If anything behaves differently, send a screenshot.

Version 0.28 also fixes two lab details from 0.27: the H/L flag now uses the patient's own range when a test's range is given for men and women separately (Hb), and older results also show in the Results Inbox.

Test on the demo hospital (hospital.royceltd.com), not on your live site, because the tests create visits, bills and stock movements.

How it works day to day: [Pharmacy and stock guide](../06-pharmacy-and-stock.md), Step 4, and the [Outpatient guide](../02-outpatient.md), Step 8.

## Before you start

You need four logins (Royce can create them for you on the demo):

| Login | Roles | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reception | Receptionist | Check-in |
| Doctor | Physician | Prescribing |
| Pharmacy | Pharmacist and Stock User | The Pharmacy Worklist |
| Cashier | Cashier | Bills and payments |

On the Pharmacy Worklist, set **Pharmacy store** to the store your demo stock is in (*Stores*).

Write down the patient, visit (VIS-…), bill (ACC-SINV-…) and delivery note (MAT-DN-…) numbers you create.

## A. A prescription waits at the pharmacy

1. **Reception:** check in a new cash patient. **Doctor:** in the consultation, prescribe paracetamol 500 mg and amoxicillin 500 mg, and submit.
2. **Pharmacy:** open Kenya Health › Pharmacy › **Pharmacy Worklist**.
   - [ ] The patient is on *Waiting*: one row, 2 drugs, *Cash*, *2 not paid*.
3. Open the row.
   - [ ] Each drug shows the doctor's dose and duration, the quantity, the stock in the store, and *Not billed*.
   - [ ] **Dispense** is not offered.

## B. Bill, pay, dispense, labels

1. **Pharmacy:** press **Bill Drugs**.
   - [ ] "The drugs are on bill ACC-SINV-…". The drugs now show *Billed, not paid*.
2. **Cashier:** open the patient's visit, submit the bill and take the payment.
3. **Pharmacy:** **Refresh** and open the row.
   - [ ] Both drugs show *Paid* and are ticked.
4. Change paracetamol's instructions to add "after food", then press **Dispense**.
   - [ ] The medicine labels open: patient, drug, quantity, the instructions (with "after food"), and the batch and expiry for a batch-tracked medicine.
   - [ ] Both drugs show *Dispensed*; the row moves to the *Dispensed* tab.
   - [ ] The *In store* figure went down by the quantities dispensed.

## C. Part now, the rest later

1. Prescribe a new patient 9 paracetamol; bill and pay as in B.
2. **Pharmacy:** set the quantity to **4** and press **Dispense**.
   - [ ] The drug still shows *Waiting (part)*, with *Dispensed* 4.
3. Dispense the remaining 5.
   - [ ] *Dispensed*.
4. Try dispensing more than is left (type a larger quantity before the last step on another patient).
   - [ ] Refused: "only … left to dispense".

## D. Buy outside and send back

1. Prescribe two drugs for a new patient (don't bill them).
2. **Pharmacy:** tick one, press **Buy Outside**, reason "Out of stock".
   - [ ] It shows *Buy outside*. **Print Prescription** opens the doctor's prescription.
   - [ ] On the visit, **Bill › Create Bill** no longer includes that drug.
3. Tick the other, press **Send Back to Doctor**, "Patient allergic, please change".
   - [ ] It shows *Sent back to doctor*. The visit is back at *Consultation*, waiting for the prescriber, with your note on the movement.
4. **Doctor:** prescribe a substitute in a new consultation.
   - [ ] The substitute appears on the pharmacy worklist for the same visit.
5. Tick a drug marked *Buy outside* and press **Back to Waiting**.
   - [ ] It is *Waiting* again.
6. Bill a drug, then try **Buy Outside** on it.
   - [ ] Refused: "already on a bill".

## E. Returns

1. Open the patient from B (*Dispensed* tab), tick paracetamol, set the quantity to **2**, press **Return** and confirm.
   - [ ] "Returned to stock". *Dispensed* goes down by 2 and *In store* goes up by 2.
2. Try returning more than was dispensed.
   - [ ] Refused.

## F. Counter sale

1. **Pharmacy:** press **Counter Sale**. Leave *Patient* blank, type a walk-in name, add paracetamol 10 tablets with instructions "2 tablets when needed", press **Send Bill to Cashier**.
   - [ ] "Bill ACC-SINV-… is waiting at the Cashier". The sale is on *Waiting*, marked *Counter sale*, *Bill at the Cashier*.
2. **Cashier:** open the bill (customer *Walk-in Customer*), submit and take the payment.
3. **Pharmacy:** open the sale and **Dispense**.
   - [ ] The label shows the walk-in's name and the instructions.
4. Repeat with a registered patient chosen in *Patient*.
   - [ ] The bill goes on the patient's visit for today (a new *Pharmacy only* visit if they had none).

## G. Lab fixes from 0.27

1. Enter an Hb of **13.0** for a **male** patient on the Lab Worklist.
   - [ ] **L** is shown (the male range starts at 13.5). For a female patient, 13.0 has no flag.
2. Open the Results Inbox and untick *Unread only*.
   - [ ] Lab results from before 0.27 are listed too.

## H. Who sees what

- [ ] The Pharmacy login sees the Pharmacy Worklist; it can make bills but not submit them or take payments.
- [ ] The Cashier and Doctor logins don't see the Pharmacy Worklist.
