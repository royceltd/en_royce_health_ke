# The Drug Screen on the Consultation and the Ward — Testing Guide

This is a short testing script for version 0.52.0. It covers the drug screen built to your design (page 3 of the feedback of 20 November): **Add Prescription** on the left, **Current Prescription** on the right, the **Medication Mapping** note and **Save Prescription**. The same screen is on the consultation and on the ward's **Drug Administration** tab. It also covers what you asked for on page 4 of the earlier document: drug class, stock, drug packages, buy externally, chronic medicines, and the pending, sent, active and past lists.

Test on the demo, **hospital.royceltd.com**. Use a Doctor login, a Nurse login for part E and a Pharmacist login for part F. Tick each item as you go, and send a screenshot if anything is different.

## Before you start

- **A patient:** use one who has paid the consultation fee and is in the Doctor's Queue.
- **A consultation:** open it, fill the Observation tab and a provisional diagnosis.
- **Part C needs a drug package.** In Healthcare Setup, open **Treatment Plan Template**, make *Malaria (adult)* and add two drugs to its **Drugs** table, with dose, frequency, duration and route.

## A. One drug on the consultation (Doctor)

1. Open the **Prescription** tab.
   - [ ] **Add Prescription** is on the left. **Current Prescription** is on the right, with a list at the top (*Pending, Sent, Active Prescriptions, Past, Chronic*).
2. Choose a **Drug Class**, then type in **Drug Name**.
   - [ ] Only that class's drugs are offered, each with *Available: N*.
   - [ ] Once chosen, a green or red **Available** badge shows its stock.
3. Fill in **Dose** *5*, **Dose Unit** *ml*, **Frequency** *TID*, **Duration** *10*, **Route** *Oral*. Keep **Start Date** and **Start Time**.
   - [ ] **Quantity** and **Dispensing Unit** fill in, for example *2* *Bottle* for a 100 ml bottle.
   - [ ] A green **Medication Mapping** note says how it is dispensed, for example *… will be dispensed as 2 Bottle, not 150 ml*.
4. Change **Quantity** to *1*, then **+ Add Prescription**.
   - [ ] The drug is in the table under **Pending**: #, Drug Name, Dose, Frequency, Duration, Route, Quantity *1 Bottle*, with view and delete.
   - [ ] The form clears and keeps the drug class.
5. Tick **Schedule dosage**, put *Every 8 hours*, and add a second drug.
   - [ ] Its frequency shows *every 8 Hour*.
6. Click the eye on a row.
   - [ ] Its details open. Click the bin on the other row.
   - [ ] It is removed.

## B. Draft and Save Prescription (Doctor)

1. Close the consultation and open it again.
   - [ ] The pending drug is still there: drugs added are kept as a draft. The Pharmacy Worklist does not show it yet.
2. Click **Complete Consultation**.
   - [ ] It is refused: *These drugs are still in draft…*.
3. **Save Prescription**.
   - [ ] *1 drug(s) sent to the pharmacy*. The **Sent** list shows it as *Waiting*, and the Pharmacy Worklist shows it.

## C. A drug package, buy externally and chronic (Doctor)

1. Click **Drug Package**, choose *Malaria (adult)*, then **+ Add Prescription**.
   - [ ] Both drugs of the package are under **Pending**.
2. Add a drug with **Buy Externally** ticked and another with **Chronic medicine** ticked, then **Save Prescription**.
   - [ ] Under **Sent**, the first shows *Buy Outside*: it isn't billed and the pharmacy doesn't dispense it.
3. Complete the consultation. On the patient's next visit, open the Prescription tab.
   - [ ] **Active Prescriptions** lists the drugs still within their course.
   - [ ] **Chronic** lists the chronic one with **Continue**. Clicking it fills the form again.

## D. The ward (Doctor)

1. Open the **Ward**, an admitted patient, then **Drug Administration**.
   - [ ] On the left: **Prescription**, **Dispensing**, **Administration Log**.
2. **Prescription**: the same screen as the consultation. Add a drug every 8 hours for 2 days, starting at 14:00, then **Save Prescription**.
   - [ ] *1 drug(s) sent to the pharmacy*. The drug is under **Sent today**.
3. **Dispensing**.
   - [ ] The drug shows 5 doses waiting for the pharmacy: today 14:00 and 22:00, tomorrow 06:00, 14:00 and 22:00.
   - [ ] **Stop**, with a reason, stops the doses not given.

## E. Administration Log (Nurse)

1. Same patient › **Drug Administration** › **Administration Log**.
   - [ ] The treatment sheet lists each dose, given or not, and by whom. **Medication Round** opens the dose recording.
   - [ ] On **Prescription**, a nurse sees the lists but not the **Add Prescription** form.

## F. Pharmacy (Pharmacist)

1. Open the patient from part A on the **Pharmacy Worklist**.
   - [ ] Each drug shows the dose, frequency, duration and route as the doctor wrote them, and the quantity from the screen.
