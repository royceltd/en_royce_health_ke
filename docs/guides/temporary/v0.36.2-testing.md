# Create Bill Fix and Cover Price Books — Testing Guide

A short script for version 0.36.2 and your notes of 17 November:
- **Create Bill** with every charge ticked works again (it failed with an error on 0.36.1);
- each cover has its own price book (for example Mwalimu), and cash patients pay the cash price (KES 200 consultation);
- a cash patient pays at the Cashier, then moves to triage;
- the lab sees a cash patient's request only once it is paid.

Each test says what to do and what the system should do. Tick each one off. If anything behaves differently, send a screenshot.

Test on your own system (**bethesda.royceltd.com**) with your staff logins. Use new test patients with "Test" in the name.

## Before you start (manager, once)

Your system has the cash price book (**Standard Selling**) with the consultation (*GEN-CONSULT*) at KES 200. Add one cover price book to test with:

1. Stock › **Price List** › *+ Add*: name **Mwalimu**, tick *Selling*. Save.
2. Stock › **Item Price** › *+ Add*: Item *GEN-CONSULT*, Price List *Mwalimu*, Rate (for example) **1,000**. Save.
3. Healthcare › **Insurance Payor**: open (or add) the insurer for Mwalimu cover.
4. Healthcare › **Insurance Payor Eligibility Plan** › *+ Add*: the Mwalimu plan for that insurer, **Price List** = *Mwalimu*. Save.

- [ ] Kenya Health Settings › **Payment Before Service**: *Consultation fee before triage* and *Laboratory* are ticked.

## A. Create Bill with every charge ticked (the error you reported)

1. **Reception:** check in a test patient: *Outpatient*, Payment *Cash*. Save.
2. **Cashier:** open the visit › **Bill** › **Create Bill**. Leave **every charge ticked** and click **Create Invoice**.
   - [ ] The invoice opens with every charge on it. No error.
3. Do it again on another visit with two charges, and **untick one**.
   - [ ] The invoice has only the ticked charge. The unticked one stays on the visit's bill for later.
4. Untick **everything** and click **Create Invoice**.
   - [ ] Refused with "Tick at least one charge to bill."

## B. Cash price book: KES 200, paid before triage

1. **Reception:** check in a test patient: *Outpatient*, Payment *Cash*. Save.
   - [ ] The visit waits at the **Cashier**, not at Triage.
   - [ ] The consultation fee on the visit's bill is **KES 200**.
2. **Cashier:** **Create Bill** › **Create Invoice**. Take the payment on the invoice and submit it.
   - [ ] The visit moves to **Triage, waiting** ("Consultation fee paid").
   - [ ] **Nurse:** the patient is on the Triage list.

## C. Cover price book: Mwalimu

1. **Reception:** check in another test patient: *Outpatient*, Payment *Insurance*, the Mwalimu insurer and plan, member number and expiry. Save.
   - [ ] The visit goes **straight to Triage**: an insured patient does not pay first.
2. After the consultation, open the visit's **Bill**.
   - [ ] The consultation is priced at the **Mwalimu** price (1,000 in the example), not 200.

## D. Lab sees only paid requests (cash)

1. **Doctor:** for the cash patient of part B, request a lab test and submit the consultation. **Send To** › *Cashier*.
2. **Lab:** open the **Lab Worklist**.
   - [ ] The test is **not** listed. A note says a patient is paying at the Cashier.
3. **Cashier:** **Create Bill** › **Create Invoice**, take payment, submit.
4. **Lab:** refresh the Lab Worklist.
   - [ ] The test is now listed.

For the Mwalimu patient of part C, a lab request appears at the lab straight away.
