# Version 0.61: Casualty as a Department, and Starter Services — Testing Guide

This testing script covers version 0.61.0, your feedback of 23 November:

- **Casualty works like the Lab and Radiology.**
  - Patients are sent to Casualty as a request, from triage or from the doctor.
  - Casualty receives them and manages them on the patient screen: diagnosis, notes, drugs from the pharmacy, requests and results.
  - Casualty then discharges the patient or admits them to a ward.
  - Casualty has its own section in the sidebar, next to Laboratory and Radiology.
- **Casualty beds are not charged** for now.
- **Your system now has a starter service list.**
  - It covers lab tests, imaging, ward and minor procedures, and theatre operations.
  - Each has a placeholder price, so requests can be made, billed and routed while you prepare your real price list.

Test on your own system, **bethesda.royceltd.com**, or on the demo. Press **Ctrl+Shift+R** first, so your browser loads the new version. Tick each item as you go, and send a screenshot if anything is different.

## A. Sending a patient to Casualty

### A1. From triage (Triage Nurse)

1. Open the patient's visit, press **Send To**, choose **Casualty**, and write a note (for example *RTA, head injury*).
2. In the sidebar, open the **Casualty** section, then **Casualty**.
   - [ ] The patient is under **Sent to Casualty** with your note.

### A2. From the consultation (Doctor)

1. On a consultation, press **Send Requests** or **Complete Consultation**.
   - [ ] In **Where does the patient go next?**, **Casualty** is one of the choices.
2. Choose **Casualty** and send.
   - [ ] The patient is under **Sent to Casualty**.

## B. Receiving and managing the patient (Casualty Nurse and Doctor)

1. On the Casualty list, press **Receive** and choose a casualty bed.
   - [ ] The patient's screen opens with the casualty tabs.
   - [ ] The patient is under **In Casualty**.
2. Record vital signs, a diagnosis, a nursing note and a doctor's note. Send a lab request.
   - [ ] Each one shows in its tab.
3. On **Casualty Drugs**, prescribe a drug and save. The pharmacy sends it, and the nurse gives it from **Administration Log** › **Give Dose**.
4. On **Billing**:
   - [ ] there is no charge for the casualty bed;
   - [ ] the lab tests and drugs are on the bill.
5. Finish with either:
   - **Disposition** › **Admit to Ward**, then the ward presses **Receive** under **Incoming transfers**; or
   - **Discharge**, as on the ward.

On your system, the **Casualty** unit has 4 beds, CAS Bed 01 to 04. Tell us how many you need and what they are called. You can also add them yourself in **Healthcare Service Unit**.

## C. The starter services (Doctor, Lab, Cashier)

Your system now has these services, each with a placeholder cash price:

- **26 lab tests:**
  - malaria slide and mRDT, Hb, Full Haemogram;
  - blood sugars, HbA1c;
  - urinalysis, stool, pregnancy test, blood group;
  - Widal, Salmonella and H. pylori antigens, Brucella, VDRL, HBsAg;
  - CRP, UEC, LFT, lipids, uric acid, TSH;
  - HIV test and sputum AFB/GeneXpert, both free.
- **12 imaging exams:** chest, abdomen, skull, spine, pelvis and limb X-rays, and 6 ultrasounds (including obstetric).
- **Ward procedures:** dressing, injection, cannulation, catheter, NG tube, nebulisation. These go to Nursing.
- **Minor procedures:** suturing, I&D, POP, ECG, foreign body removal. These go to the Procedure Room.
- **8 theatre operations:** Caesarean section, appendicectomy, herniorrhaphy, laparotomy, MVA, tubal ligation, circumcision, hysterectomy. These go to Theatre.

1. On a consultation's **Requests** tab, choose each Request Type and open **Service**.
   - [ ] The services are listed with their price.
2. Send a lab test, an X-ray and a theatre operation.
   - [ ] Each reaches its department: Lab Worklist, Radiology Worklist, Theatre Requests.
   - [ ] Each is on the patient's bill at its price.

**Before real billing:** these prices are placeholders, and each service's description says so. Send us your price list (cash, SHA and other insurers) and we will load it. You can also change a price yourself: open **Item Price** and filter by the item.
