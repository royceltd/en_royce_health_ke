# Your Feedback of 17 November: What Changed and How to Set Up Services — Guide

Your IT team raised four points on 17 November. This guide says what each one became, how to check it, and how to set up the services the doctor requests, with their prices and categories. Versions 0.41 and 0.42 are on the demo (**hospital.royceltd.com**) and on your own system (**bethesda.royceltd.com**).

**Test with the role logins** (Reception, Cashier, Nurse, Doctor), not Administrator. Administrator skips some checks by design, so it doesn't show what each department sees.

| Your point | What we did | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The system lets a patient go on before paying | A consultation, or triage vital signs, for a cash patient whose fee is unpaid is now refused everywhere, not only through Send To. The form says why as soon as the patient is chosen. | Part A |
| Triage and consultation on their own menu; triage shouldn't see unpaid patients | **Triage Queue** and **Doctor's Queue** (and a **Cashier Queue**) in the menu. Each shows only the patients sent to that station, so unpaid patients, who wait at the Cashier, don't appear. | Part B |
| Price book for each service (e.g. Full Haemogram cash 1,500, another price 500) | Already supported: one price per price list (price book). Set up below. | Part C |
| Categories: dressing under nursing, haemogram and urinalysis under lab, X-ray and ultrasound under radiology | Categories are item groups, set up below. The doctor's Requests tab already separates lab tests, imaging and procedures. | Part C |

## A. Nobody sees the patient before paying

**Settings (manager, once):** Kenya Health Settings › Payment Before Service:
- [ ] **Consultation fee paid first**: ticked.
- [ ] **Consultation fee is paid**: *Before triage* (your choice). Hospitals that triage first can choose *Before the doctor*.
- [ ] **Consultations only for checked-in patients**: ticked.

**Check it:**
1. **Reception:** check in a cash test patient (*Outpatient*). The visit waits at the **Cashier**.
2. **Doctor:** Consultation › **+ Add** for that patient.
   - [ ] A red notice at once: the consultation fee is not paid. **Save** is refused.
3. **Nurse:** Vital Signs › **+ Add** for that patient, and Save.
   - [ ] Refused: the fee is not paid.
4. **Doctor:** a consultation for a patient **not checked in today**.
   - [ ] Refused: check the patient in at reception first.
5. **Cashier:** take the fee (visit › Bill › Create Bill › Create Invoice, save, **Take Payment**).
   - [ ] The patient moves to the **Triage Queue** by themselves; the nurse can now record vital signs, and the doctor can consult.

Still seen before paying, as before: insured patients, **Casualty** visits, inpatients, and a patient a manager let through (visit › Bill › **Allow Services Before Payment**).

## B. Your own queue in the menu

Kenya Health menu:

| Menu | Opens | For |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Triage › Triage Queue** | Patients sent to Triage (paid, or allowed) | Nurses |
| **Triage › Vital Signs** | All vital signs records | Nurses |
| **Consultation › Doctor's Queue** | Patients sent to Consultation; press **My Patients** for yours | Doctors |
| **Consultation › Consultations** | All consultations | Doctors |
| **Billing › Cashier Queue** | Patients waiting to pay | Cashiers |

Each person sees only the menus their role can open (a cashier doesn't see Vital Signs or Consultations). The lab, radiology and pharmacy keep their worklists.

**Check it:**
- [ ] **Nurse:** Triage Queue lists only patients at Triage. The cash patient of part A appears only after paying.
- [ ] **Doctor:** Doctor's Queue › **My Patients** lists your patients at Consultation.
- [ ] **Cashier:** Cashier Queue lists the patients waiting to pay.

A queue for another station (for example a procedure room) can be added from the sidebar settings without new software: a link to Patient Visit filtered by that station. Ask us if you want one.

## C. Services: categories and price books

Every service the doctor can request is an **item** with a **category** (its item group) and a **price in each price book** (price list). The doctor orders it from the consultation's **Requests** tab: lab tests, imaging and procedures each have their own section.

### C1. Categories (once)

Stock › **Item Group** › *+ Add*:

| Item Group | Parent | For |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Laboratory | All Item Groups | already there: Full Haemogram, Urinalysis… |
| Radiology | All Item Groups, tick **Is Group** | the parent of the two below |
| X-ray | Radiology | X-ray exams |
| Ultrasound | Radiology | Ultrasound scans |
| Nursing | All Item Groups | Dressing, injections, nursing procedures |

### C2. The services, with their cash price

- **Lab tests:** Healthcare › **Lab Test Template** › *+ Add*: name (*Full Haemogram*), **Item Group** *Laboratory*, **Is Billable** ticked, **Rate** *1,500*. The rate becomes the price on the cash price book (*Standard Selling*). Do the same for *Urinalysis*.
- **X-ray and ultrasound:** Healthcare › **Observation Template** › *+ Add*: name (*Chest X-ray*), **Observation Category** *Imaging*, **Modality** *X-ray* (or *Ultrasound*), **Item Group** *X-ray* (or *Ultrasound*), **Is Billable** ticked, **Rate**. They appear on the Radiology Worklist when ordered.
- **Nursing:** Healthcare › **Clinical Procedure Template** › *+ Add*: name (*Dressing*), **Item Group** *Nursing*, **Is Billable** ticked, **Rate**. The doctor orders it under Procedures.

### C3. Another price book

The cash price is on *Standard Selling*. A second price for the same service (your "Full Haemogram 500") goes on its own price book:
1. Stock › **Price List** › *+ Add*: a name for who pays that price (for example *Mwalimu*), tick **Selling**. Save.
2. Stock › **Item Price** › *+ Add*: Item *Full Haemogram*, Price List *Mwalimu*, Rate *500*. Repeat for each service that scheme prices differently. (Or open the price list and use **Import** for a whole tariff.)
3. Link the price book to whoever pays it: for an insurance scheme, Healthcare › **Insurance Payor Eligibility Plan** › the plan › **Price List** *Mwalimu*.
4. The **Item Prices** report shows each service's prices side by side.

A patient on that scheme is billed 500 automatically; a cash patient, 1,500.

**Check it:**
- [ ] **Doctor:** request *Full Haemogram* for a cash patient: the visit's Bill shows **1,500**.
- [ ] The same for a patient on the scheme: **500**.
- [ ] Request a *Dressing*: it shows under Procedures, priced from *Nursing*.
