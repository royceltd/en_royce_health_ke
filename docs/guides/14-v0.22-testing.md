# Consultation, Pricebooks, Cashiers and Insured Billing — Testing Guide

A short script to test the changes Bethesda asked for on 11 November 2026 (version 0.22): the residence list, the Consultation form and medical history, pricebooks per scheme, insured patients billed automatically, cashiers and their shifts, the turnaround time report, and the maternity package rule. Each test says what to do and what the system should do. Tick each one off. If anything behaves differently, send a screenshot.

Test on the demo hospital (hospital.royceltd.com), not on your live site, because the tests create patients, visits and invoices. The steps are explained in full in the guides named in each section.

## Before you start

You need five logins (Royce can create them for you on the demo):

| Login | Roles | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reception | Receptionist | Registration and check-in |
| Doctor | Physician | Consultations |
| Lab | Laboratory User, Accounts User | Lab tests; trying to take payment outside the cashier |
| Cashier | Cashier | Bills, payments and shifts |
| Manager | Healthcare Administrator, Accounts Manager, System Manager | Settings, prices, reports |

Demo records used below: doctor **Dr Amina Achieng** (consultation fee KES 1,500), service type **General Consultation**, insurer **Mlima Health Assurance (Demo)** with scheme **Corporate Gold (Demo)** (covers 90%), lab tests *Malaria Blood Slide (BS for MPS)*, *Haemoglobin (Hb)* and *White Cell Count (WBC)*. Write down the patient, visit (VIS-…) and invoice (ACC-SINV-…) numbers you create.

## A. Residence list

Guide: [Reception, queue and billing](12-reception-queue-billing.md), Step 1.

| # | Do this | The system should |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | As **Reception**, Healthcare › Patient › *+ Add Patient*. Choose county **Nakuru**, then click **Residence**. | The list shows Nakuru's places (Nakuru town, Milimani, Lanet, Kiamunyi, Bahati, all marked demo) and places with no county. |
| 2 | Pick **Milimani (demo)**. | The sub-county fills in (Nakuru Town West). |
| 3 | On a second new patient, type a place that isn't listed (for example *Free Area*) in Residence and choose **Create a new Residence**. Give it type *Estate* and save it. | The new place is saved with the county you chose on the patient, and is picked on the patient. |
| 4 | Type a residence that isn't on the list and save the patient without creating it. | Refuse: the residence must be on the list. |
| 5 | As **Manager**, Kenya Health › Residence. Open *Free Area* and correct its spelling (rename). | The patients linked to it follow the new name. |
| 6 | Kenya Health › Reports › **Patients by Residence**, this month. | Each place with the patients registered and seen, busiest first, and a chart. Patients without a residence show as *(not recorded)*. |

## D. Insured patients billed automatically

Guide: [Insurance](04-insurance.md), *How an insured patient's bill is made and settled*.

| # | Do this | The system should |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 18 | As **Manager**, check Kenya Health Settings › Payment Before Service: **Bill insured visits automatically** is ticked. | Ticked (the default). |
| 19 | Check in a new insured patient (Mlima, Corporate Gold). As **Doctor**, order a **Malaria Blood Slide** in the consultation and submit. | Nobody presses Create Bill, yet the visit's Bill section shows a draft invoice with the consultation and the slide, each with the insurer's share. |
| 20 | Start a review consultation for the same visit, order **Haemoglobin**, submit. | The same draft invoice gains the review and the Hb test; the visit still has one invoice. |
| 21 | As the **Lab**, start the malaria slide before anything is paid. | It starts: insured patients are served first. |
| 22 | As **Reception**, **Status › Complete Visit**. | The visit closes and its invoice is **submitted** (signed by KRA eTIMS). The invoice shows the insurer's share, and the copay (10%) is what the patient owes. |
| 23 | As the **Cashier**, take the copay on that invoice. | The invoice is Paid; the insurer's share waits for the claim. |

## E. A booked appointment is charged once

| # | Do this | The system should |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 24 | As **Reception**, book an appointment for a cash patient with Dr Amina Achieng for today. Then on the appointment press **Start Visit** and save the visit. | The appointment is *Checked In*. Booking alone did not create a visit. |
| 25 | As **Doctor**, on the visit **Record › Consultation**, submit it. Open the visit's **Bill**. | **One** consultation line (1,500), on the appointment. Before version 0.22 it showed two. |
| 26 | Repeat 24–25 for an insured patient whose appointment was booked **without** insurance, checking them in on Insurance. | One consultation line, with the insurer's share. |

## F. Cashiers and shifts

Guides: [Cashier day-end](07-cashier-day-end.md), *Cashier shifts*; [Administrator setup](08-administrator-setup.md), Step 1.

| # | Do this | The system should |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 27 | As **Manager**, Kenya Health Settings › **Cashiers**: tick *Only cashiers take payments* and *Payments need an open cashier shift*. Save. | Saved. |
| 28 | Make an unpaid bill for a cash patient (check in, consult, **Bill › Create Bill**, submit). As the **Lab**, open that invoice. | No **Create › Payment** and no *Include Payment* box: the lab can bill but not receive money. |
| 29 | As the **Cashier**, open the same invoice and try **Create › Payment** before opening a shift. | Refuse: *Open your cashier shift first*. |
| 30 | Kenya Health › **Cashier Shift** › *+ Add*. Your name fills in. Opening Float **1,000**. Save. Now take the payment in **Cash**. | The payment is saved and shows the shift. |
| 31 | Try to open a second shift for yourself. | Refuse: you already have an open shift. |
| 32 | Open your shift and press **Refresh Takings**. | Cash: 1 payment, expected = 1,000 float + the payment. |
| 33 | Enter **Counted** 50 less than expected. Press **Close Shift**. | Refuse until you write a note: the difference is −50. With a note, the shift is **Closed** with the closing time. |
| 34 | As **Manager**, Kenya Health › Cashier Shift. | Managers see every cashier's shifts; a cashier sees only their own. |
| 35 | Untick both Cashier settings again, unless the hospital wants them on. | |

## G. Turnaround time

Guide: [Outpatient](02-outpatient.md), Step 6.

| # | Do this | The system should |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 36 | As **Manager**, open the Lab Test Template **Malaria Blood Slide (BS for MPS)** and set **Target Turnaround (minutes)** to **60**. | Saved. |
| 37 | Check in a cash patient, order a malaria slide, bill and pay it. As the **Lab**, create the lab test, enter a result and submit. | The lab test shows a **Result Time** to the second. |
| 38 | Kenya Health › Reports › **Turnaround Time**, last 7 days. | Per test: the number done, median, average, the time 90% were done within, the target and the share within target, with a chart. |
| 39 | Tick **Each Request**. | Each test with ordered, sample collected, received, result and the minutes between them. *Within Target* is red when over 60 minutes. Tests done before version 0.22 are marked approximate. |
